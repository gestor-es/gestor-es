## Summary

- RETA is a personal obligation of the autónomo. It is not a tax of the SL. Since 01.01.2023 the system of rendimientos reales applies. The forecast sets the provisional base. AEAT data set the later TGSS regularización.
- For an ordinary autónomo in estimación directa, the starting amount is **rendimiento neto fiscal + own RETA cuotas that were deducted**. Then **7% gastos genéricos** is deducted. This is not simply revenue minus bank expenses.
- For an autónomo societario who meets the 90-day rule, **3%** applies, not 7%. The remuneration from the relevant company and certain dividends also count. SL profit kept in the company does not by itself become personal rendimiento.
- The 2026 table is confirmed by **Orden PJC/297/2026, art. 18**, with effect from 01.01.2026. The tramo limits and minimum bases stay as in 2025. The G11/G12 maximum rose from 4.909,50 to 5.101,20 €. This is not a full freeze of cuotas.
- The full ordinary rate is **31,40% in 2025** and **31,50% in 2026**. This includes cese de actividad **0,90%**, FP **0,10%**, and MEI **0,80% / 0,90%** respectively.
- For societarios and familiares colaboradores, the 2025 minimum is **base 1.000 €**. The final 2026 minimum is **1.424,40 €** if the 90-day rule is met. Keeping the provisional base of 1.000 € in 2026 does not exempt from an additional payment.
- **Beckham needs separate control**: we found a TGSS Valencia clarification. It says that standard data from modelo 151 did not arrive and that a technical base of 1.000 € was applied in previous regularizaciones. This is not a reliable nationwide calculation rule for 2025/2026. It is not proof of exemption from rendimientos reales.
- Cuota reducida: 80 € is fixed by law for 2023–2025. MEI is added separately. The currently indexed Importass guía for 2026 states 80 € + MEI and a total of 88,64 €. But the legal basis for exactly 80 € in 2026 and the arithmetic of this total do not fully match the norms found. See "Uncertainties". Do not promise a fixed debit without a TGSS decision.
- The base can be changed up to six times a year. The change takes effect on 1 March, May, July, September, November and January of the next year. A change in December does not fix the December cuota.
- A regularización below the minimum means an additional payment by the last day of the month after notification. A regularización above the maximum means a refund. If the average provisional base is within the permitted range, a voluntary higher base is not refunded down to the minimum.
- Moving between autonomous communities does not change the national RETA rates. Regional subsidies are a separate issue. Russian citizenship, the visa de emprendedor and Beckham do not by themselves cancel RETA.
- For 2027, no new tramo table was found in the checked primary sources (**UNVERIFIED**). MEI **1,00%** is already set. Do not use draft proposals from negotiations as law in force.

## Rules

1. **Who must register.** RETA applies to personal, direct, habitual self-employed activity. It also applies to an administrador/socio with efectivo control in the cases of art. 305 LGSS. For control, not only the direct 25%/33% matter, but also the presumptions of family control. An administrador without control may belong to the Régimen General asimilado, not to RETA. One RETA registration covers several own activities; their rendimientos are added together. Low income by itself is not an automatic statutory exemption from alta. **Period:** 2025–2026; the rendimientos reales system applies since 01.01.2023. **Sources:** [LGSS, arts. 305, 308](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11724#a305), [RDL 13/2022, art. primero](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-12482#ap).

2. **Ordinary SS rendimiento.** For estimación directa, take the IRPF rendimiento neto before individual reducciones. Add own SS/mutualidad alternativa cuotas. Take into account the reverse adjustment of regularizaciones of past years. Then apply 7%. The IRPF deduction for gastos de difícil justificación in estimación directa simplificada is already part of the rendimiento neto. It is not cancelled just because SS also applies 7%. IVA repercutido and retención IRPF do not reduce the income from the activity. The retención is a tax prepayment. **Period:** 2025–2026; main rule since 01.01.2023. **Sources:** [LGSS, art. 308.1.c](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11724#a308), [AEAT: exact casillas for SS](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/empresarios-individuales-profesionales/nuevo-sistema-cotizacion-autonomos/informacion-determinar-rendimiento-neto.html).

3. **Societario: company income and 3%.** Include own professional profit. Include rendimientos íntegros, dinerarios/en especie, from participation in the fondos propios of companies in which, on the IS devengo date, the person holds ≥33% of the capital, or ≥25% for an administrador. Also include income from work in these companies. Dividends are included **before withholding**, even though in ordinary IRPF they belong to ahorro. Do not automatically add dividends from any stock-market portfolio, sales of shares or undistributed SL profit. For art. 305.2.b/e the 3% deduction applies with ≥90 days of this status in the regularized period. An ordinary colaborador under art. 305.2.k does not get the 3% only because of the general minimum floor. **Period:** 2025–2026, since 01.01.2023. **Sources:** [RDL 13/2022: text of art. 308.1.c, reglas 1–2](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-12482#ap), [AEAT: societarios and modelos 190/193](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/empresarios-individuales-profesionales/nuevo-sistema-cotizacion-autonomos/informacion-determinar-rendimiento-neto.html).

4. **Minimum base for societario/colaborador.** With ≥90 days of the relevant status, a special floor applies, even if income is negative or below the SMI: 1.000 € for 2025; 1.424,40 € for 2026. For 2026 the Orden allows keeping the 2025 provisional base, but the final regularización uses the grupo 7 minimum. This is a base, not a mensualidad to pay. A recognised cuota reducida is assessed separately from the ordinary minimum. **Period:** separately 01.01–31.12.2025 and 01.01–31.12.2026. **Sources:** [Orden PJC/178/2025, art. 18.4](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2025-3780#a1-10), [Orden PJC/297/2026, arts. 3, 18.4](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2026-7296#a1-10), [BNR 05/2026, pp. 9–10](https://www.seg-social.es/wps/wcm/connect/wss/61f4db39-8366-4d8f-863b-34332b1d527a/BNR+5_2026.pdf?MOD=AJPERES).

5. **Beckham has no published RETA tariff of its own.** Art. 308 refers to the normas IRPF. But you cannot mechanically take the gross income of modelo 151 as casilla 0224 of ordinary IRPF. You also cannot deduct expenses that the special tax regime does not allow. A reply from the Dirección Provincial TGSS Valencia was published by a professional association in February 2026. It states the following. With modelo 151, rendimientos do not arrive in the standard AEAT exchange. «en estos momentos» a base of 1.000 € is set. The question was passed to the Subdirección General. The document concerns the regularización of past years that was current at that time. It does not create a permanent advantage for Beckham. BNR 05/2026 directly states a general fallback of 1.424,40 € for 2026 when there is no return or exchange income. But modelo 151 is not specifically resolved. **Period:** practice observed in February 2026; applicability to the 2025/2026 regularización is **UNVERIFIED**. **Sources:** [TGSS Valencia, p. 5, copy on the association's website](https://www.cograsova.es/images/adjuntosmail/2026/20260226/consultas.pdf), [BNR 05/2026, p. 10](https://www.seg-social.es/wps/wcm/connect/wss/61f4db39-8366-4d8f-863b-34332b1d527a/BNR+5_2026.pdf?MOD=AJPERES), [LGSS, art. 308](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11724#a308).

6. **Cuota reducida / tarifa plana.** Condition: initial alta, or no alta in the previous 2 years; 3 years if the benefit was already used. Apply at alta. The law speaks of the period from the alta date and the following 12 full calendar months. So do not replace the TGSS decision with a simple 365-day counter. The next 12 full months are granted on prior application before the continuation starts, if annual rendimientos netos are below the annual SMI. If the continuation spans two years, the condition is checked in each year. The first period cannot be regularized because of high income. If the income condition of the second period is lost, the affected part is regularized. Societarios can use the benefit; colaboradores cannot. With discapacidad ≥33%, violencia de género/terrorismo, the periods are 24 + 36 months. Do not equate the amount of the special second period with 80 € without checking. Cese/FP are not charged during the cuota reducida period. MEI is not bonificable. **Period:** scheme since 01.01.2023, in force in 2025–2026; the amount of 80 € is expressly set for 2023–2025. **Sources:** [RDL 13/2022, art. tercero / art. 38 ter LETA and DT quinta](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-12482#at), [DT quinta](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-12482#dt-5), [Importass guía](https://portal.seg-social.gob.es/wps/portal/importass/importass/Colectivos/trabajo%2Bautonomo/guia), [LGSS art. 127 bis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11724#a1-5).

7. **Other bonificaciones do not equal a discount on the whole cuota.** Colaborador with no alta for 5 years: 50% for the first 18 months and 25% for the next 6 months, on the CC cuota on the minimum base G1. Nacimiento/adopción/riesgo embarazo/lactancia: 100% of the relevant CC without IT, calculated on the average base of the previous 12 months. An autónoma who returns within 2 years after stopping for the listed family reasons: 80% of that CC for 24 months. Cuidado menor cáncer/enfermedad grave: 75% of CC without IT during the recognised prestación. Conciliación vinculada a contratación: up to 12 months; 100% of CC without IT if the conditions of art. 30 are met; 50% when the replacement worker has permitted part-time hours. It is not a general benefit for having a child. **Period:** rules for 2025–2026; grounds and calculation updated since 01.01.2023. **Sources:** [LETA arts. 30, 35, 38, 38 bis, 38 quater](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-13409#a30), [RDL 13/2022, art. tercero](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-12482#at). For the actual amount of the benefit, keep the TGSS resolución and the base/excluded IT component. Do not subtract percentages from 31,4/31,5%.

8. **Change of base.** No more than six effective changes per year. The application also contains a new forecast of the average monthly annual rendimiento. You can choose any base inside the permitted range. It does not have to be the minimum. **Period:** since 01.01.2023, including 2025–2026. **Source:** [RDL 13/2022, art. quinto, art. 45 Reglamento](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-12482#aq).

9. **Regularización is an annual process, not an additional payment per invoice.** TGSS receives AEAT data. It excludes non-regularizable periods. It determines the average provisional base and the actual tramo. If the average base is within min/max, TGSS keeps the provisional bases. If it is below/above, TGSS recalculates the regularizable months to min/max and adds up the positive and negative differences. The rates of the relevant past period apply, not those of the notification year. Payments for which the base was already used to grant a prestación, and the listed benefit periods, have special exclusions. **Period:** the wording of art. 46 is in force since 01.08.2024 and applies to 2025–2026. **Sources:** [RD 665/2024, full new art. 46](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2024/07/10/pdfs/BOE-A-2024-14086.pdf), [TGSS manual, p. 6](https://www.seg-social.es/wps/wcm/connect/wss/9334b014-b8de-45d4-8b0c-c76e44486853/Manual%2Bregularizacion%2BRETA%2BSistema%2BRED_Opcion%2Bpor%2Bbase%2Bsuperior_v2.pdf?MOD=AJPERES).

10. **Payment and late payment.** The ordinary cuota is paid for the current month. Domiciliación is debited on the last bank working day of the month. If the liquidación duties were met: recargo 10% for payment in the first following calendar month, 20% later. If the duties were not met: 20% until the end of the reclamación/acta period and 35% after it. Intereses and loss of benefits are possible. This is not the AEAT scale for a late tax return. An additional regularización payment made within its special period carries no recargo/intereses. **Period:** 2025–2026. **Sources:** [official Revista Seguridad Social](https://revista.seg-social.es/-/si-eres-aut%C3%B3nomo-puedes-consultar-cada-mes-tu-cuota-a-la-seguridad-social-de-manera-telem%C3%A1tica/1000), [TGSS: recargos](https://www.seg-social.es/wps/portal/wss/internet/Trabajadores/CotizacionRecaudacionTrabajadores/10721/10957/583?changeLanguage=es), [LGSS arts. 30–31](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11724#a30).

11. **Deduction in ordinary IRPF.** Own cuotas are a gasto deducible. With simultaneous income as administrador and from own activity, a single cuota can be assigned to the work or to the activity. It cannot be deducted twice. An additional regularización payment is an expense of the year it is paid. A refund reduces the expense of that year; the excess over expenses is income. This is no reason to redo the original return for the year of the provisional cuotas. **Period:** 2025–2026; the casillas below are confirmed by the 2025 form/manual. **Source:** [AEAT Manual IRPF 2025, gastos del titular](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/gastos-fiscalmente-deducibles/gastos-titular-actividad.html). For Beckham, the deduction does not carry over automatically from ordinary IRPF.

12. **The SL pays the administrador's RETA.** The individual remains the obligated person. If the SL bears the cost, this is remuneration, usually en especie. If the SL only transfers an amount withheld from cash remuneration, do not create a second remuneration. For IS, the expense is assessed as real documented remuneration: contabilización, devengo, justification of the services, proper corporate decisions and the operaciones vinculadas rules where applicable. It is not an automatic gasto de Seguridad Social empresarial. If the payment is handled as a préstamo/cuenta con socio with repayment, there is no IS expense. You cannot automatically deny the deduction only because of a defect in the estatutos while ignoring case law. Disputed cases need separate qualification. **Period:** 2025–2026. **Sources:** [LIS arts. 10, 11, 15.e, 18](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-12328), [AEAT Manual IS 2025: liberalidades](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/manual-sociedades-2025/capitulo-05-liquidacion-is-determinacion-imponible/bi-antes-reserva-capitalizac-compensac-00550/detalle-correcc-result-cuenta-perdidas-is/gastos-no-deducibles/gastos-donativos-liberalidades.html), [DGT V0067-19, official address; text not obtained directly — UNVERIFIED on the primary site](https://petete.tributos.hacienda.gob.es/consultas/?num_consulta=V0067-19).

## Formulas for the engine

### Data and classification

Use Decimal, with amounts in euros. Do not compute money with binary float. Store separately: accrual year, month, alta status, calendar days of alta, TGSS días regularizables, provisional base, paid recibo, cuota components, benefits and their recognition periods, AEAT declaración and certificado de rendimientos, TGSS notification date, and the date of the additional payment or refund. Keep **separate** `tax_regime` and `social_security_status`: Beckham is not a type of RETA.

```text
inputs:
  year ∈ {2025, 2026}
  ordinary_IRPF | Beckham
  individually_exercised_activities
  societario_status_days, colaborador_status_days
  applicable_company_ownership_at_IS_devengo, administrator_role
  AEAT tax results, actual/provisional RETA cuotas
  TGSS regularizable periods, bases, coverage and benefits
outputs:
  computable_annual_income, R_monthly, tramo
  allowed_base_min/max, recommended_base, estimated_monthly_cuota
  estimated_regularization, cash_reserve, confidence/status

if Beckham and no written TGSS determination for relevant year:
  status = REQUIRES_TGSS_CRITERION
  use recognized TGSS base/recibo for actual payment ledger
  show ordinary-art308 simulation and missing-data-floor scenario separately
  do not label either scenario as legally final; do not silently set income=0
```

### Ordinary IRPF: link with modelo 100

For each activity, then add them up:

```text
A_directa = casilla[0224] + casilla[0186] + casilla[0196] - casilla[0197]
```

This is the mapping published by AEAT. `[0224]` already includes the permitted IRPF deduction for gastos de difícil justificación. Do not replace it with the rendimiento neto reducido. Do not add the cuotas a second time if the input is already given as rendimiento antes de RETA. When a refund exceeds the current cuotas, part of it is in `[0178]`. Reconcile such an anomaly with the IRPF certificado and with the income that AEAT actually sent. Do not subtract the same amount twice.

```text
A_societario = qualifying_gross_remuneration_from_company
             + qualifying_gross_equity_distributions
# If RETA is already included in remuneration en especie, do not add it again.
# Do not subtract retenciones, the personal cuota or 2.000 € gastos trabajo from these gross amounts.

A_total = sum(A_directa) + A_societario + other_art308_computable_income
p = 0.03 if qualifying_art305_b_or_e_days >= 90 else 0.07
N = A_total - A_total * p
```

A negative result does not turn into "no return filed". These are different legal states. For an ordinary autónomo, a negative R falls into R1, not into the 1.000/1.424,40 € fallback.

### Average monthly income, range, recommendation

Art. 44/46 Reglamento sets `R = N / D_regularizable × 30`. `D_regularizable` is the number of days that TGSS counts after exclusions. It is not simply the number of paid invoices. For a full year, the forecast `N/12` is convenient to show separately. But it cannot be declared the exact regularización formula without a caveat: the literal formula of the norm uses days. For an ordinary full calendar year 2025/2026, use `N/365×30`. In an imported TGSS calculation, use its confirmed day counter. The practical difference from the conventional 360-day year used in some materials is noted below.

```text
if D_regularizable == 0:
  income_regularization = 0
  keep recognized nonregularizable bases
else:
  R = N / D_regularizable * 30          # do not round before choosing tramo
  (lo, hi) = table[year].lookup(R)      # exact <, <=, >= as in the table
  if special_floor_applies:
    lo = max(lo, floor[year])
    hi = max(hi, lo)                   # the special floor is not blocked by a low tramo
  recommended_base = lo               # goal: minimum mandatory payments
  if user_explicitly_wants_higher_benefit_base:
    selected_base = chosen amount in [lo, hi]
  else:
    selected_base = recommended_base
```

Forecast the year from the actual figures since the start of the year plus the expected remaining income and expenses. Include one-off bonuses and dividends of a societario. Near a limit, show the range of possible tramos. Do not advise an artificially low forecast. A base change takes effect only in the next permitted window. Do not raise the minimum "just in case" with a hidden margin.

### Cuota and rounding

```text
ordinary_rate[2025] = .283 + .013 + .009 + .001 + .008 = .314
ordinary_rate[2026] = .283 + .013 + .009 + .001 + .009 = .315
estimated_full_month_cuota = ROUND_HALF_UP(selected_base * rate, 2)
```

Store the calculation of each component without early rounding. Store the official recibo separately. The order of rounding of individual components or differences in the internal TGSS system, down to the cent, is not confirmed by a primary technical document. The tables and examples round the total monthly amount. The final amount to pay comes from the TGSS documento de ingreso. Do not present a self-calculated amount as an official payment document.

Partial month and non-regularizable intervals: use the prorated base under the liquidación rules and the actual TGSS días. Do not automatically charge a full month for one day. But remember that the favourable daily alta/baja is limited to three cases per year. For cuota reducida, use the recognised fixed part + MEI on the applicable base. Do not add cese/FP to it and do not apply the ordinary 31,4/31,5% to it.

### Regularización

Do not compute `sum(clamp(base of each month))`. Under the norm, the **average base** of the whole regularizable period is compared first.

```text
B_sum = sum(provisional bases attributable to regularizable periods)
B_avg = B_sum / D_regularizable * 30       # art46; take normalized TGSS data
(lo, hi) = permitted interval for actual R and special category

if no_IRPF_or_no_reported_income_condition_confirmed_by_TGSS:
  target_base = missing_data_floor[year]  # 1000 / 1424.40; do not choose by zero profit
elif lo <= B_avg <= hi:
  preserve each provisional base
  income_related_delta = 0
elif B_avg < lo:
  target_base = lo
else:
  target_base = hi
  apply eligible 31-12-2022 retention option only if expressly chosen

if target_base exists:
  for each regularizable liquidation period m:
    delta_base_m = prorated(target_base,m) - provisional_base_m
    delta_cuota_m = apply(period_specific_rates_and_bonifications, delta_base_m)
  income_related_delta = sum(delta_cuota_m)

estimated_result = income_related_delta + separately_identified_TGSS_corrections
positive -> estimated additional payment
negative -> estimated refund
cash_reserve = max(0, estimated_result)
```

Art. 46.2.7 requires applying the rates and conditions to the **base differences**. For a to-the-cent forecast, you may round the monthly difference. But compare the result with the official recibo or decision. Note that debt for unpaid provisional cuotas, recargos and intereses does not disappear automatically when the final base is lowered. Separate corrections of status or coverage are possible even for periods that already have a definitive base.

Calculate the pluriactividad refund **after** the main regularización:

```text
excess = max(0, CC_RETA_paid + CC_employee_paid + CC_employer_paid - annual_threshold)
refund_pluriactividad = min(excess * .50, CC_RETA_paid * .50)
```

## Edge cases

- **One invoice per month / foreign client.** The client's country, reverse charge, no IVA on the invoice and withholding of Spanish IRPF do not by themselves change RETA. You need the income as recognised for tax, not the net bank inflow.
- **IRPF simplificada and 7%.** These are two different deductions. Take the fiscal resultado after the permitted gastos de difícil justificación, then apply SS 7%. Do not replace the 5% tax deduction with 7%. Do not count the SS cuotas twice.
- **90 days societario.** Determine the classification, the 3% deduction and the minimum floor from the annual status data, not only from the status on 31 December. Do not automatically extend the ownership threshold for including dividends to all ways of proving efectivo control.
- **Year with a switch from autónomo to SL.** Income from the individual activity and qualifying remuneration/dividends may be added together. One RETA applies, but you need the status dates and a single 90-day check. Two registered activities do not mean two cuotas.
- **Colaborador without own salary.** Lack of personal income does not cancel the special minimum. Do not mix up missing exchange data with a negative resultado that was actually declared. Familiares colaboradores usually do not apply their own estimación directa to the activity of the main autónomo.
- **Prestaciones / IT.** After 60 days of IT, with the relevant right, the mutua/SEPE pays the cuotas. The months of payments and the bases used to calculate the prestación may become no regularizables. Do not automatically reserve an ordinary additional payment for each such month. Source: [LGSS art. 309](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11724#a309).
- **Pluriactividad.** The salary from an ordinary third-party employer does not become RETA income only because the individual is also an autónomo. First comes the regularización, then the refund of the excess CC. This is not an exemption from RETA because of full contributions to the Régimen General.
- **Income at a limit.** Exactly 1.166,70 € → G1; exactly 900 → R2; exactly 4.050 → G10. Compare the unrounded calculation. Round the display separately. Keep a TGSS decision with its own rounding as a fact.
- **High base that existed before 2023.** The option to keep it requires the conditions of DT sexta and a timely choice. Do not offer it to someone who first registered in 2025.
- **Moving within Spain.** There is no separate regional RETA scale. A regional cuota cero or subsidy is a separate payment with conditions and a possible tax qualification. Do not reduce the national cuota before it is granted. Update the domicilio in TGSS and in AEAT separately.
- **Russian passport.** A España–Rusia agreement on Seguridad Social exists. It has been in force since 22.02.1996. The usual five-year desplazamiento scheme applies to employees por cuenta ajena. An autónomo does not get an automatic exemption; agreed otras excepciones with E/R.2 may be possible for them. Without confirmed applicable coverage, do not exclude RETA. [TGSS: Federación de Rusia](https://www.seg-social.es/wps/portal/wss/internet/InformacionUtil/32078/32253/1437/1440/119870?changeLanguage=es).
- **Additional payment received after leaving the activity.** The tax qualification of the regularización follows the nature of the previous activity or work. You cannot charge it to a new SL only because the payment arrived after the SL was registered. An accounting expense for a forecast future additional payment is not the same as a tax expense that the individual has already paid.

## Uncertainties

1. **Beckham: blocking uncertainty for an automatic final regularización.** A specific TGSS Valencia reply on modelo 151 was found. But it is posted on an association's website. It is not a general BOE/DGT legal act. It describes the practice of a past regularización. No published nationwide answer was found on which tax net yield to take for an art. 93 entrepreneur and how to send it from modelo 151. Do not promise that it is "always only the 1.000 € base"; for 2026 the national fallback is already 1.424,40 €. Cautious implementation: a written request to TGSS/CASIA with the year and the specific status; store the reply and the resolución. Until then, mark forecasts as **UNVERIFIED** and record actual payments by recibos. This is not a reason to file a wrong modelo 100.

   On the **tax** side there is a specific verified limit. The [AEAT modelo 151 instructions, epígrafe B](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/impuesto-sobre-renta-personas-fisicas/modelo-151-decla_____los-trabajadores-desplazados-espanol_/instrucciones-cumplimentacion-ejercicio-2023-siguientes.html) allow, under art. 24.2 TRLIRNR, only gastos de personal `[03]`, aprovisionamiento de materiales and suministros `[04]`. Cargas sociales of staff are not the entrepreneur's personal RETA cuota. So do not include own RETA in these expenses and do not transfer `[0186]` of ordinary modelo 100 there. The fact that 151 has its own "rendimiento neto" does not decide which value TGSS must receive for art. 308.

2. **Cuota reducida 2026 and MEI: conflict between primary materials.** DT quinta RDL 13/2022 expressly limits the 80 € to 2023–2025. From 2026 it refers to the LPGE. In the RDL 3/2026 and Orden PJC/297/2026 that were studied, no separate setting of 80 € was found. At the same time, the indexed official Importass guía publishes 80 € + 0,9% MEI = 88,64 €. But the minimum G1 in the Orden is 950,98 €, which gives 88,56 €. The amount of 88,64 € matches the old base of 960,60 €. The legal basis for this MEI basis and for the amount for a new alta in 2026 is **UNVERIFIED**. The service shows this discrepancy and uses the recognised alta/recibo. It does not derive the 88,64 € amount from an invented formula.

3. **Regularización days.** BOE art. 44/46 and the RED manual set division by the counted days of alta, multiplied by 30. Some professional summaries use 360 days for a full year instead of the literal 365 calendar days. No accessible primary technical algorithm for normalizing all full and partial months was found. Do not declare a simple `annual/12` a universal legal algorithm. Inside the engine, you need separate confirmed `D_regularizable` and normalized bases from TGSS. For a forecast, store both scenarios if a limit is near. The 4.000 € example is chosen on purpose to give the same tramo with both counters. The exact equivalence of a self-made calendar counter with TGSS data is **UNVERIFIED**.

4. **Rounding to the cent.** The rates and bases are checked. The table products are calculated. The exact order of rounding for CC/CP/cese/FP/MEI, partial months and differences is not confirmed by a TGSS technical specification. So the amounts are an estimación; the official settlement is the documento de ingreso. Do not add a hidden tolerance that turns a real debt into zero.

5. **Tax casilla numbers for 2026.** At the research date, the manual and schema of the future Renta 2026 campaign were not confirmed. The formulas are kept separate from the annual form mapping. `[0186]/[0196]/[0197]/[0224]` are confirmed by the current AEAT page and by Renta 2025. But a future exporter must load the approved form. It must not copy the form blindly.

6. **IS and administrador.** The general deducibilidad rules are confirmed by LIS/AEAT. But the full text of the specific DGT V0067-19 was not obtained from the official site: the URL returned a search engine shell. The statement about the exact wording of this consulta is marked **UNVERIFIED**. The primary sources confirm the separate deductibility of the personal cuota in ordinary IRPF. When the SL pays, you need proper qualification of the remuneration, the ingreso a cuenta and corporate documents. A universal switch "SS for the director is deductible" is not enough.

   Further confirmation was found in the full text of **DGT V1796-25 of 13.10.2025**, reproduced by [Fiscal Impuestos, pp. 2–3](https://www.fiscal-impuestos.com/sites/fiscal-impuestos.com/files/V1796-2025.pdf). Paying RETA for a socio-trabajador creates mayor retribución. With direct payment, it is en especie with ingreso a cuenta (added to the income valuation if it is not passed on to the recipient). When money is given, it is dineraria. At the same time, the cuota is deducible for the individual under art. 19 LIRPF. This is an accessible copy of the DGT text, but **not the primary host**. It supports the IRPF qualification. It is not a separate decision on the IS deduction. Remuneration for ordinary work other than the administrador functions does not automatically get clave E or the administrador rate.

7. **2027 and regional ayudas.** An adopted 2027 RETA table was not found. The final regional cuota cero amounts for a given person were not found either. MEI 1% is confirmed; new limits/base/SMI are **UNVERIFIED**. The engine must not apply draft laws or a conditional extension without a source version and an effective date.
