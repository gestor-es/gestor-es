# Immigration status, taxes and Seguridad Social

Check date: **08.10.2026**. This is a rule base for foreign nationals (including citizens of the Russian Federation) who work in Spain as an autónomo or through an SL. It also covers moving between autonomous communities. The examples are illustrative. This is a rule base for the service. It is not an opinion on whether a specific application is admissible. Immigration status, tax residence, the régimen especial del art. 93 LIRPF and the Seguridad Social regime must be assessed **separately**.

## Summary

| Status | Work and territory | Beckham for an autónomo | Usual duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Residencia para emprendedores**, Ley 14/2013, arts. 69–70 | A real innovative project / special economic interest for Spain, informe favorable ENISA; all of Spain | Possible on the separate ground of **actividad emprendedora**, not simply because of registration as an autónomo. The causal link with the move, the documents and art. 93 are checked | Initially **3 years**, renewal **2 years** |
| **Teletrabajo de carácter internacional**, autónomo | Remote professional work for foreign companies; Spanish professional activity is no more than **20%** of the total; all of Spain | **Does not arise automatically.** The direct mention of the digital nomad visa in the tax rule refers to **trabajadores por cuenta ajena**. An ordinary freelancer usually does not qualify without another art. 93 ground | Visa up to **1 year**; residencia up to **3 years**, then **2 years** each time |
| **Residencia temporal y trabajo por cuenta propia**, régimen general | The initial permit is limited to a Comunidad Autónoma and a sector; after renewal: all of Spain, any sector, cuenta propia and cuenta ajena | Ordinary self-employed activity by itself **gives no right**; a separate qualifying ground is checked independently | Initially **1 year**, renewal **4 years** |
| **UE/EEE/Suiza / another confirmed right to work without these restrictions** | No 20% limit and no link to ENISA solely because of citizenship; registration obligations remain | EU citizenship does not exclude art. 93, but it does not grant it automatically | For EU citizens: registro when living in Spain for more than 3 months; after 5 years, if the conditions are met, residencia permanente |

**Two corrections of dangerous assumptions:** "digital nomad autónomo = Beckham" is wrong. "There is no convenio de Seguridad Social between Spain and Russia" is also wrong. The agreement exists, but it does not exempt every Russian sole proprietor (IP) from RETA.

## Rules

1. **Applicable version and different procedures.** The teletrabajadores regime and the new emprendedores structure apply after Ley 28/2022, from **23.12.2022**. The tax changes to art. 93 apply from **01.01.2023**. For the régimen general, **RD 1155/2024** applies from **20.05.2025**, not the former RD 557/2011. The consolidated version of RD 1155/2024 with the update of **22.09.2026** was checked. The issue date of an old permit and the transitional provisions matter: an existing card does not change its term automatically. Sources: [Ley 14/2013](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2013-10074), [Ley 28/2022](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-21739), [RD 1155/2024](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2024-24099&p=20260922&tn=1).

2. **General Beckham conditions, from 01.01.2023.** The person must acquire Spanish tax residence as a result of a qualifying move. The person must not have been a Spanish tax resident in the **five preceding tax periods**. The person must have a ground provided in art. 93 and meet the other conditions. AEAT explains that the check of the five periods is based on the **year in which tax residence is acquired**, not mechanically on the year of physical entry. The move must take place in the first year of application of the regime or in the previous year. Example: residence for the first time in 2026 → check 2021–2025. The regime applies in the year of the change of residence and in the five following periods. It does not mean six years from the TIE date. The option is usually filed with **Modelo 149 within six months** from the start date of the activity shown in the applicable alta SS document / document confirming that foreign coverage is kept / other provided confirmation. The annual return is **Modelo 151**. Getting a new visa, opening an SL or moving between regions while already living in Spain does not restart these deadlines. Sources: [AEAT, ámbito de aplicación, IRPF 2025](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c02-irpf-cuestiones-generales/sujecion-irpf-aspectos-personales/regimen-fiscal-especial-aplicable-trabajadores-desplazados/regimen-aplicable-trabajadores-desplazados/ambito-aplicacion.html), [Modelo 149 instructions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/impuesto-sobre-renta-personas-fisicas/modelo-149-irpf-comunicacion-opcion-exclusion_/instrucciones-cumplimentar-comunicacion.html).

3. **Beckham for each ground.**
   - **Emprendedor:** art. 93.1.b).3.º allows an actividad económica calificada como emprendedora under art. 70 Ley 14/2013. A favourable ENISA report is needed. An arbitrary startup name or an ordinary alta as autónomo is not enough. AEAT refers to an autorización de residencia obtained before the relevant move. For EU citizens and persons with the right of free movement, a separate informe ENISA is required before the move. The immigration option to apply while already in Spain does not by itself prove a tax right to the regime.
   - **Digital nomad autónomo:** the favourable presumption of art. 93.1.b).1.º is worded for workers **por cuenta ajena** with a teletrabajo visa. The professional services of an autónomo must be assessed on separate grounds: entrepreneurial activity, or the provided services of profesionales altamente cualificados for empresas emergentes / formación, investigación, desarrollo e innovación. For the relevant ground, the threshold of **más del 40%** of the income provided by law is checked, among other things. You cannot simply apply 24% to all freelancers with a DN.
   - **Cuenta propia:** ordinary consulting or development work as an autónomo is not an art. 93 ground. An entrepreneurial ground or another exception must be proven separately. The type of TIE does not replace the tax check.
   - **UE/other:** the analysis is the same. Citizenship by itself is neither a ground nor a bar. There is a separate art. 93 branch for the director of an SL. However, services of the company owner that differ from the functions of an administrador can amount to a non-permitted economic activity.
   - For everyone: art. 93.1.c) prohibits income through an establecimiento permanente in Spain, except for the expressly permitted entrepreneurial/professional grounds. Do not mix this tax test with the choice of the SS regime.

   Source and current interpretation: [AEAT, conditions of art. 93 and art. 113 RIRPF](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c02-irpf-cuestiones-generales/sujecion-irpf-aspectos-personales/regimen-fiscal-especial-aplicable-trabajadores-desplazados/regimen-aplicable-trabajadores-desplazados/ambito-aplicacion.html).

4. **Digital nomad: activity limits, from 23.12.2022.** Arts. 74 bis–ter Ley 14/2013 require the following. Work must use exclusively remote means. The foreign company/group must have had real and continuous activity for at least **1 year**. The professional relationship with one or more foreign companies must have existed for at least the **last 3 months**. The conditions for continuing to work remotely must also be met. Qualification: suitable higher/professional education or at least **3 years of professional experience**. For the initial application, keep the contracts, start dates, evidence of the clients' activity and the permissions/conditions for working from Spain. The three months are not a requirement of time in RETA. They are also not a requirement that every new client after the permit is issued already has a three-month history.

   For an **actividad profesional**, the Spanish part must not exceed **20% del total de su actividad profesional**. The foreign activity must continue. The person cannot switch completely to Spanish clients. For an **actividad laboral**, work is allowed only for companies outside Spain: the 20% limit does not carry over to a Spanish employer. The law does not set a "revenue over a rolling 365 days" formula in this article. The monetary share and the rolling year are useful service indicators. They are not a safe harbour set by law. Sources: [arts. 74 bis–quinquies](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2013-10074#a7-2), [UGE, ficha titulares](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/documents/d/unidadgrandesempresas/informacion-documentacion-pagina-web-titular-v2), [UGE FAQ](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/documents/d/unidadgrandesempresas/nomadas-digitales-faqs-espanol).

5. **Emprendedor: consistency with the project, from 23.12.2022.** Under art. 70, the following are assessed: the profile and involvement of the applicant, the **plan de negocio**, the product/service, the financing, and the innovation / economic interest for Spain. This article sets no general mandatory minimum investment and no minimum number of jobs to be created. The financing must match the project. The activity must actually keep the conditions on which the permit was granted. A pivot of the business model, the end of the project or its replacement by ordinary freelance work require a separate assessment. The status does not continue automatically. For renewal, UGE describes a procedure for applying again for an informe ENISA. Do not treat every deviation from the plan as an automatic loss of the TIE. The authority decides after the established procedure. Sources: [arts. 69–70](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2013-10074#a69), [Instrucción DGM 1/2023, 03.04.2023](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/documents/410169/0/report_230403%2BInstrucciones%2BENISA_corregido%2Bcobertura.pdf/730e8460-4a51-e2f6-ee17-cc330c9def8a?t=1681813814762), [UGE, solicitud inicial and renovada](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/documents/1823432/1826098/Instrucciones%2Bsolicitud%2Bemprendedores.pdf/3a4d0b60-71bf-1c53-9f9d-fff7f94cbf5d?t=1682675484705).

6. **Cuenta propia: territory and renewal, from 20.05.2025.** Art. **83** RD 1155/2024: the initial permit is for one year and is limited to a Comunidad Autónoma and a sector. Art. 85.6: activity in several autonomous communities in the first year requires the corresponding permits. Art. **87**: the renewed permit is for four years and allows work por cuenta propia and por cuenta ajena in any region and sector. Therefore, moving the actual activity **to another Comunidad Autónoma before the first renewal** cannot be treated as free just because the person already has an NIE/RETA. Check the resolución and whether a modificación/additional permit is needed. A client address in another region is not by itself the same as moving the place of activity.

   Initially, the following are checked: whether the investment in the project is sufficient, qualification/experience, and colegiación and licences where needed. Art. 84 of the new Reglamento **does not set a universal monetary minimum of "100% IPREM for every autónomo"**. On renewal, the usual ground is continuation of the activity and a check of taxes/SS. There are also the alternatives of art. 86.2: suitable support from a family member, recognised protección por cese de actividad, certain TRADE cases and other provided grounds. Source: [RD 1155/2024, arts. 83–87](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2024-24099&p=20260922&tn=1#a8-5).

7. **Taxes and SS on renewal of cuenta propia.** Art. 86.2.a) and 86.5 provide for a check of obligations **de oficio**. Debts for SS contributions do not automatically exclude renewal if habitual/regular activity is proven. The information is passed to the Inspección de Trabajo y Seguridad Social. This does not write off the debt and does not permit non-payment. A negative certificado is a warning and a reason for review. It is not a ready conclusion that "renewal is prohibited". For DN/emprendedor, Ley 14/2013 requires that the conditions are kept. However, the text of the law sets no separate universal requirement to attach specifically Modelo 100/151 or both certificados to every renewal. Sources: [art. 86](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2024-24099&p=20260922&tn=1#a8-8), [Hoja 15, update May 2025](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/15.-renovacion-de-la-autorizacion-de-residencia-temporal-y-trabajo-por-cuenta-propia), [art. 76 Ley 14/2013](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2013-10074#a76).

8. **Seguridad Social depends on the real work, not on the name of the visa.** A DN with professional activity must register in **RETA** after obtaining the permit and **before starting the activity in Spain**. The exception is a valid exemption under international rules with a suitable certificado de legislación aplicable/cobertura. For an emprendedor and a cuenta propia holder who work personally as an autónomo, the usual result is also RETA. With an SL, functions, control and shareholding are analysed. You cannot automatically assign RETA to all holders of an entrepreneur visa. You also cannot cancel it automatically after a company is set up. Private health insurance does not replace mandatory contributions. Separate proof of health coverage may be needed even with foreign SS. Beckham does not exempt from RETA. Sources: [UGE, documentación titulares](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/documents/d/unidadgrandesempresas/informacion-documentacion-titulares), [DGM 1/2023](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/documents/410169/0/report_230403%2BInstrucciones%2BENISA_corregido%2Bcobertura.pdf/730e8460-4a51-e2f6-ee17-cc330c9def8a?t=1681813814762), [RD 1155/2024, art. 85](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2024-24099&p=20260922&tn=1#a8-7).

9. **Russia: the convenio exists, there is no automatic RETA exemption.** The Convenio de Seguridad Social España–Federación de Rusia was signed on **11.04.1994** and has applied since **22.02.1996**. The general rule is the legislation of the country of work. TGSS states directly that the usual scheme of temporary posting for up to five years applies to **por cuenta ajena**. Autónomos are posted under the **otras excepciones** mechanism, with agreement of the competent authorities. The TGSS page describes posting **from Spain to Russia** and the documents E/R.1, E/R.2. They cannot automatically be declared documents for posting in the opposite direction. For a Russian autónomo working from Spain, the following are **not sufficient**: payment of Russian contributions, a Russian sole proprietorship (IP) or a Russian passport. Treat the practical availability and acceptance of a specific Russian certificate for a DN in 2026 as **UNVERIFIED** until TGSS/UGE confirms it. Without a confirmed ground, the service must not switch off RETA. Sources: [BOE, convenio and acuerdo administrativo](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-1996-4208), [TGSS, desplazados Rusia](https://www.seg-social.es/wps/portal/wss/internet/InformacionUtil/32078/32253/1437/1440/119870?changeLanguage=es).

10. **UE/"no restrictions" does not mean no status.** For UE/EEE citizens, under art. 7 RD 240/2007, work por cuenta propia is a ground for living in Spain for more than three months. Registration within the established three-month period is required. For an economically inactive EU citizen, sufficient resources and health insurance are checked, not the DN threshold of 200% SMI. A Russian citizen cannot choose "UE/none" just to switch off the checks. A specific document/ground is needed, for example residencia de larga duración or the applicable right of a family member. The restrictions and terms of such a document are taken into account separately. Sources: [RD 240/2007](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2007/02/16/240/con), [Orden PRE/1490/2012, resources and proof of activity](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/o/2012/07/09/pre1490/con).

11. **Tax residence is a separate counter.** Art. 9 LIRPF: **more than 183 days** in a calendar year, or the main centre of activity/economic interests in Spain. There is a rebuttable family presumption. Sporadic absences can be included in the count unless tax residence in another country is proven. Therefore, "fewer than 184 physical days" does not guarantee non-residence. A TIE also does not prove residence automatically. Dual residence requires a separate analysis of the applicable agreement. Source: [AEAT, residencia personas físicas](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/manual-tributacion-no-residentes/capitulo-01-contribuyente-residencia/residencia-personas-fisicas.html).

12. **Larga duración and nacionalidad: different clocks.** Larga duración nacional usually requires **five years of legal continuous residence**. Absences of up to **six consecutive months** do not break continuity if in total they do not exceed **ten months in five years**. For work reasons, the limit is up to **eighteen months in total**, with the necessary proof. Force majeure is assessed individually. This is not an automatic grant after five years of residence. It is also not a tax residence rule. For a Russian citizen, the usual period before nacionalidad por residencia is **ten years**, unless there is a personal ground for a shorter period. Legal, continuous residencia immediately before the application is required. The larga duración absence rules must not be presented as safe limits for citizenship. Sources: [Hoja 49, arts. 182–185 RD 1155/2024](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/49.-autorizacion-de-residencia-de-larga-duracion-nacional), [Ministerio de Justicia, nacionalidad por residencia](https://www.mjusticia.gob.es/es/ciudadania/tramites/nacionalidad-residencia).

- **SMI 2025:** [RD 87/2025](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2025-2576), in force from 13.02.2025, economic effects from 01.01.2025. **SMI 2026:** [RD 126/2026](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2026-3815), in force from 20.02.2026, economic effects from 01.01.2026. The retroactive effect of the wage SMI does not mean automatic cancellation of a residence permit already issued. Whether the new threshold applies to a specific case is checked under the procedure.
- **DN:** the UGE instructions and ficha use the formula **200% SMI mensual**, plus 75% for the family and 25% for each further member. [Consulado Buenos Aires](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Consulados/buenosaires/es/ServiciosConsulares/Paginas/index.aspx?scca=Visados&scco=Argentina&scd=40&scs=Visados+Nacionales+-+Visado+de+residencia+para+teletrabajo+%28n%C3%B3mada+digital%29) states directly **2 442 € mensuales durante 14 pagas**. [Consulado Washington](https://exteriores.gob.es/Consulados/washington/en/ServiciosConsulares/Paginas/Consular/Telework-visa.aspx) publishes a monthly 2 442 €. Therefore, 2 442 × 12 cannot be presented without a caveat as the annual equivalent of 200% SMI. For budgets and warnings, also show 34 188 €/year and 2 849 €/month when spread over 12 months. Do not claim that every authority directly publishes exactly the last figure.
- **Emprendedor:** the same percentages of 200% + 75% + 25% are set by [Instrucción DGM 1/2023, section on medios económicos](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/documents/410169/0/report_230403%2BInstrucciones%2BENISA_corregido%2Bcobertura.pdf/730e8460-4a51-e2f6-ee17-cc330c9def8a?t=1681813814762). These are resources to support the applicant/family. They are **not a minimum startup turnover and not a substitute for project financing**. Show personal resources and business investment separately.
- **Initial application and renewal of DN/emprendedor:** whether sufficient resources are kept is assessed against the current SMI and the family composition. Do not fix the threshold forever at the year of the first residence permit. As proof, prepare current contracts, invoices, receipts into the bank account and documents on available legal resources. The following require checking against the current ficha/consulate: whether savings can make up an income shortfall, how the required coverage is calculated for the whole term, and how gross/net is treated for a specific autónomo. **Do not treat an account balance as a substitute for the mandatory foreign activity of a DN**.
- **Cuenta propia:** the new art. 84 requires sufficient investment in the specific project, not one amount for everyone. On ordinary renewal: a viable continuing activity and a tax/SS check, not a universal "200% SMI is mandatory". If the renewal relies on support from a family member, the separate rules of that ground apply.
- **UE:** for an autónomo who actually works, there is no universal DN minimum of 200% SMI. For an economically inactive person, resources are assessed taking into account the personal/family situation.
- **IPREM:** the base is set by [Ley 31/2022, disposición adicional nonagésima](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-22128). The table [Exteriores, IPREM 2025/2026](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Consulados/losangeles/en/ServiciosConsulares/Documents/IPREM.pdf) keeps 600 / 7 200 / 8 400. This is a reference indicator for procedures that refer to it. It is **not an automatic threshold for all four statuses**. The rule must not replace SMI with IPREM.

## What the service must do

### General settings and checks

- Separate the fields: **ciudadanía**, the exact residencia ground, **visado or autorización**, initial/renewal, issue/start/end date, number and text of the resolución, permitted work/region/sector. Separately: régimen fiscal, the date of first tax residence, the periods before the move, alta SS and the applicable SS legislation. Keep the history; do not overwrite the old status.
- Choosing "UE/other without visa restrictions" requires a confirmed ground. If the document has not been reviewed yet, show "not checked", not a green "you may work".
- **Do not switch on Beckham based on the visa type.** For an ordinary DN-autónomo, show "no automatic ground". Collect separate evidence for art. 93 and the result of Modelo 149. For a user who already lives in Spain, first check the earlier tax years and the deadline for the option.
- Keep a calendar of entries/exits and three independent screens: the tax calendar year, continuity of residencia for larga duración, and evidence of continuity for nacionalidad. Do not give the recommendation "spending 183 days is enough".
- When the address changes, create tasks for the padrón, for updating the domicilio fiscal/AEAT correspondence, for TGSS and for immigration notifications under the relevant procedure. Do not treat a change of address as an automatic change of Comunidad Autónoma for IRPF. Store the actual days of residence by region.
- Version threshold amounts by source, period, formula and number of payments. Store the unrounded result; show cents on screen. When proving a minimum, do not round down. If an amount is between the nominal monthly threshold and the annual/12 threshold, show "the method applied needs to be checked", not a clear refusal/approval.
- Warn in advance before a contract, an SS certificate, an insurance policy or a TIE expires. An expired coverage certificate must not keep switching off the RETA task.
- Store links to evidence, the rule check date and the **UNVERIFIED** status. Automation can assemble a package and remind about a deadline. It must not claim that a permit has been renewed or a return accepted without a resguardo/result from the authority.

### Digital nomad autónomo

- For each client, store the legal name, the country where the counterparty is **actually located**, the type of relación profesional/laboral, the contract and dates. The country of the bank, the currency, the payment intermediary and the fact of IVA reverse charge do not define the client for the 20% limit.
- Dashboard: the share of Spanish activity by revenue **excluding IVA**, including rectificativas, for the last 12 months, the current calendar year and the permit period. Show issued invoices and actual receipts separately. If hours/volume of work are significant, store them as additional evidence. Dividends, sales of assets, loans and transfers between the person's own accounts do not increase the denominator of professional activity.
- If **20%** is exceeded in any observed window, show a red warning: "risk of non-compliance with art. 74 bis, review required". For example, 21 000 € of Spanish professional services out of 100 000 € in total for the rolling year → 21%, alert. A preliminary warning can be set at 18% as a product margin. It is **not a legal threshold**. A zero denominator or an unknown client country → "insufficient data", not 0%.
- Forecast the share taking into account a planned Spanish invoice **before it is issued**. Do not block the lawful issue of an invoice for work already done. Show options for a consultation/change of ground, not advice to hide a Spanish client.
- Show a separate alert when all foreign contracts disappear, when the work stops being remote or when a Spanish employment contract appears. A change of a key contract → check the UGE notification within 30 days. Do not turn the three-month rule into a ban on new clients.
- Initial application checklist: foreign company ≥1 year, qualifying relationship ≥3 months, education/experience, remote work conditions, resources, SS and health coverage. On renewal: continuity of the conditions over the whole term, current contracts and economic evidence, not only the last three successful months.

### Emprendedor

- Link the profile to a specific informe ENISA and version of the plan de negocio. Record the user's role, the company/project, the promised investments, the material results and the actual activity.
- Separate indicators: family resources by SMI; cash resources/financing of the project; fulfilment of the key conditions of the plan. Do not interpret a loss in one quarter or the absence of employees as an automatic loss of status.
- A change of the project, closure of the SL, the user leaving the project, or a material change of role → alert to assess whether the ground is kept and to notify UGE within **30 days**. ENISA for residencia and certification as an empresa emergente are different documents. They are not interchangeable flags.
- Before renewal, prepare materials for a new informe ENISA, an activity report, evidence of financing and a current budget. Separately re-check whether a new parallel activity creates a breach of art. 93.1.c).

### Cuenta propia

- For the initial permit, store the permitted Comunidad Autónoma and sector from the resolución. If the activity is planned to move **to another Comunidad Autónoma**, show a warning about a modificación/additional permit. After a confirmed four-year renewal, remove exactly these initial restrictions.
- Monitor alta RETA, licences/colegiación, actual continuation of the activity, returns and debts to AEAT/TGSS. A tax return being "filed" and the tax being "paid" are different states.
- On baja or cese de actividad, do not declare renewal impossible automatically. Show the alternatives provided in art. 86 and the documents for each. A TGSS debt is a high risk of enforcement and a reason for a check. It is not an automatic refusal under the immigration rule.

### UE / another right without these visa restrictions

- Switch off the DN 20% limit, the ENISA project and the territorial link of the initial cuenta propia **only when they really do not apply**.
- Keep the usual IRPF/IVA/SS checks, the registro UE / term of the specific card, the check of the grounds of stay of an economically inactive person and an independent Beckham analysis.

## Deadlines

| Event | Rule / task |
|---|---|
| DN visado | Up to 1 year, or less if the work is shorter; switching to an autorización is possible within **60 calendar days before the visa expires**, if the conditions are kept (art. 74 quater) |
| DN autorización | Up to 3 years, or the shorter term of the work; renewals of 2 years each (art. 74 quinquies) |
| Emprendedor autorización | 3 years + renewal for 2 years (art. 69); do not use old "2 + 2" guides or "3 + 3" web tables contrary to the BOE |
| Renewal under Ley 14/2013 | Prepare the package 120 days in advance; reference filing date: 60 days before expiry under the general UGE guide. Filing the renewal keeps the permit in force until the decision; art. 76 also allows filing in the following **90 days**, but with a possible penalty. This is an emergency window, not a recommended deadline |
| Material change of DN/emprendedor conditions | Notify UGE within **30 days**, disposición adicional séptima Ley 14/2013; keep the resguardo |
| Cuenta propia initial | 1 year. Alta SS must take place **within 3 months after legal entry**; it gives the permit effect. TIE request: within **1 month after alta** (art. 85) |
| Cuenta propia renovación | File within the **2 months before** expiry; filing within the **3 months after** is possible with a risk of penalty. These are months, not automatically 60/90 days. Renewal: 4 years; if there is no decision within 3 months, art. 87.2 provides for silencio positivo, but the service must not replace the confirming documents with its own timer |
| TIE under Ley 14/2013 | For an autorización of more than 6 months, a TIE is required (art. 76.2); show the deadline and cita according to the resolución and the Policía procedure; do not treat the visa and the TIE as one document |
| UE | Registro when living in Spain for more than 3 months: application within 3 months after entry (art. 7 RD 240/2007) |
| Beckham | Usually 6 months for Modelo 149 from the start provided by the rules, not from the start of the next Renta; a separate annual calendar for Modelo 151 |

### Renewal package

Separate the **provided condition**, the **way to prove it** and the **documents the authority may request in addition**. The following list is a preparation package. It does not claim that every item is mandatory in every case:

- The full passport, valid; the TIE/resolución, the applicable formulario, the tasa and proof of its payment; the address and family composition. For children: school attendance documents where applicable; art. 86 for cuenta propia directly regulates escolarización.
- A recent **certificado de estar al corriente de obligaciones tributarias AEAT** and a recent **certificado de estar al corriente con la Seguridad Social TGSS**, or the data to obtain/check them; a breakdown of the debt and documentos de aplazamiento, if any. For cuenta propia, the law provides for obtaining the information de oficio: do not make the user obtain extra certificates when the authority already receives them.
- **Informe de vida laboral**, proof of alta/RETA periods and of payment of contributions. With foreign coverage: a valid certificate for the relevant activity/period and proof of its acceptance. Vida laboral does not replace the certificado de estar al corriente.
- **Modelo 100 under ordinary IRPF or Modelo 151 under a valid régimen especial**, with proof of filing/payment; the applicable quarterly returns and censal alta. Do not require both annual forms for the same year as a standard. If the deadline for the first annual return has not yet arrived: actual contracts, invoices, statements and other evidence, not a fictitious return.
- For a DN: current foreign contracts, the activity history of the foreign counterparties, invoices/receipts, a report on the Spanish share and on current family resources, proof of remote work.
- For an emprendedor: materials for the new ENISA assessment, a description of the actual implementation of the plan, financial information and evidence of personal resources.
- For cuenta propia: evidence that the activity continues and of the necessary permits/qualifications. With an alternative renewal ground: the documents of exactly that ground.
- Certificados penales, apostilla/legalización and traducción jurada: according to the requirements of the specific procedure and the foreign document. Do not assume an annual obligation to get a new Russian certificate without a ground. For the initial Ley 14/2013 application, separately check the certificates for the countries of residence and the declaración responsable for the preceding period.

## Edge cases

- **The user already lives in Spain.** Switching to emprendedor or DN now is not a new move to Spain for art. 93. First find out the actual first year of residence, the earlier ground for the move and whether the option was filed on time.
- **Moving to another Comunidad Autónoma.** For DN and emprendedor, the permits apply in all of Spain. If the conditions are kept, no new visa is needed because of the region. For an initial cuenta propia, check the territory of the permitted activity. In all cases, current addresses and a separate tax analysis of regional residence are needed.
- **Exactly 20% is allowed under the wording of the law**, but a classification error/incomplete data can hide an excess. Do not treat 19,99% as a guarantee. Do not treat 20% as permission for Spanish employment.
- **Individuals, marketplace, agency and the person's own SL.** The law speaks of an empresa ubicada en España. Specific intermediary/counterparty structures require identifying the real professional activity. A Spanish bank payment is not always a Spanish client. Invoicing a foreign intermediary does not always make Spanish activity foreign. For B2C and related companies: manual review, not an automatic zero Spanish share.
- **Setting up an SL instead of being an autónomo** does not remove the restrictions of the personal work permit, RETA/company control or art. 93. The functions of administrador, the professional services of the company and dividends are analysed separately.
- **Loss of the main foreign client** requires an assessment of whether the DN conditions continue, and a notification. However, the article on the three-month relationship at the initial application does not create a mandatory three-month ban on replacing a client. Do not promise a universal "grace period without work", which the checked articles do not contain.
- **A startup loss** is not the same as a lack of resources to support the family: investments and liquid personal resources can differ from profit. However, money in the account does not replace a real innovative project.
- **Expired TIE with a renewal filed.** Keeping residencia until the decision and the ability to cross the border are different questions. Before a trip, check whether an autorización de regreso and travel documents are needed.
- **Foreign SS and health insurance.** A convenio may not cover all risks. A legislation certificate and access to asistencia sanitaria are not one document. Do not derive a RETA exemption from an insurance policy.
- **Days of absence.** The larga duración rules do not replace the conditions of the current permit. The tax counter does not replace the continuity check for citizenship. The old FAQ statement about "a maximum of six months outside Spain per calendar year" for a DN cannot be used as a confirmed current automatic ground for cancelling the TIE.

## Open questions

1. **UNVERIFIED — a single 20% method.** The checked arts. 74 bis–ter do not define the measurement period, the cash/accrual method, or whether "actividad profesional" is reduced exclusively to revenue. Rolling 12 months, the calendar year and the permit period are control screens of the service. They are not three different legal limits. For a disputed counterparty structure or a share close to 20%, a written clarification from UGE/an individual legal analysis is required.
2. **UNVERIFIED — a universal annual/12 practice for every authority.** SMI 2025/2026, the coefficients and the official consular statement of 14 payments are confirmed. The ways the threshold is published differ. Therefore, the service shows both bases and the source of the calculation applied. In particular, do not use 2 442 × 12 as a sufficient annual amount in 2026 without checking. Do not claim that 2 849 €/month is written directly in every UGE ficha.
3. **UNVERIFIED — an exhaustive mandatory DN-renovación checklist as of 08.10.2026.** The new UGE fichas found are marked as **solicitudes iniciales**. The general renewals guide of 2018 predates the DN. The list of evidence that the conditions are kept is grounded in the law. However, the following are checked against the current procedure/requerimiento: whether each separate PDF is mandatory, how recent it must be, the three-month sample of invoices and the use of savings in a specific renewal. Documents must not be collected under the false label "always mandatory".
4. **UNVERIFIED — practical Russian SS exemption for a specific applicant.** The existence of the convenio is confirmed by BOE/TGSS. The issue, scope and acceptance of a certificate for a Russian autónomo in Spain in 2026 are not confirmed. A check with the competent authorities is needed. Do not mix the social security convenio with the agreement for the avoidance of double taxation and news about its separate provisions.
5. **UNVERIFIED — the specific tax classification of an atypical DN-autónomo.** The general AEAT conclusion is clear: the teletrabajo visa ground of art. 93 is not an automatic permission for an ordinary autónomo. Non-standard administrator functions, empresa emergente services, ENISA and parallel activity require a separate check of the facts. This page does not claim that a specific consulta vinculante DGT matching all of the user's facts was found.
6. **UNVERIFIED — a universal current DN absence limit of "6 months per calendar year".** No such special limit was found in the checked law. The current indexed FAQ materials do not allow the earlier wording to be confirmed as a rule in force. Do not automate cancellation of status on that basis. Separately confirm the conditions of the specific permit and count the established periods for larga duración.
7. **Limits of the source check.** BOE, AEAT and TGSS were read directly. Some UGE PDFs returned HTTP 403. Exteriores returned HTTP 503 or incomplete PDF extraction. The corresponding points were also checked against indexed extracts of official documents. To file an application, open the current form/guide of the authority and check the version. The UNVERIFIED markers above refer to the stated gap. They do not cancel the terms confirmed by law: 3+2, 1+4, the 20% limit, the existence of the convenio or the SMI values.
