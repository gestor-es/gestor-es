## Summary

- Scope: an individual who is a Spanish tax resident, under **ordinary IRPF**, with an actividad económica in estimación directa simplificada (EDS). This is not a Beckham calculation. It is not Impuesto sobre Sociedades for an SL. Russian citizenship by itself does not change the rules. Worldwide income is taken into account.
- Checked against primary sources as of **08.10.2026**: AEAT Manual Renta 2025, the LIRPF/RIRPF in force, and the laws of the Comunitat Valenciana and Asturias. The 2026 legislation can already be used for calculation. The final formulario/casillas of Modelo 100 for 2026 cannot yet be treated as approved.
- EDS: the turnover of all actividades in the previous year is not more than **600 000 €**, and there is no incompatible method or waiver. Revenue minus supported expenses, RETA and amortización, then **5% gastos de difícil justificación**, at most **2 000 € per year per taxpayer**, in 2025 and 2026.
- Important correction to the original assumptions: your own meals in Spain are limited to **26,67 €/day without a related overnight stay and 53,34 €/day with one**, not 48,08 €. Abroad the limits are 48,08/91,35 €. This is a cap on an actual expense. It is not an automatic daily allowance.
- Art. 32.2 is for certain autónomos. It is not a general relief for every freelancer. Besides único cliente/TRADE, it requires ≥70% of income with retención and other conditions. **6 498 €**, not 7 302 €, is the additional low-income reducción. The amount of 7 302 € belongs to rendimientos del trabajo under art. 20.
- Retención for a professional: usually **15%**. For a new professional who meets the conditions: **7% in the start year and the next two calendar tax years**. Usually a Spanish B2B client withholds. A foreign client without a Spanish obligation to retener does not.
- Modelo 130 is an advance payment, not the final 20% tax. The data is cumulative from 1 January. Casilla 05 is not simply the money actually paid before. It is the positive previous casillas 07 minus the previous casillas 16.
- A professional actividad is exempt from 130 if in the previous year ≥70% of its income was objeto de retención. In the first year the relevant quarter is checked. This does not exempt you from the annual Modelo 100.
- The mínimo personal reduces the **cuota under the scale**. It does not reduce the base before the marginal rate is applied. In the example the person is under 65: estatal 5 550 €, regional 6 105 € in both regions considered.
- **Asturias changed the scale and the mínimos already for 2025**: you cannot use the old mínimo of 5 550 € and the old starting rate of 10%. **Valencia changed the scale for 2026**, and a further reduction for **2027 has already been adopted**, Ley 5/2026.
- A move does not split the annual IRPF into months of two regions. One Comunidad Autónoma is determined, first of all by the largest number of days in the year. Then its scale and mínimos apply to the whole year.
- With 60 000 € of revenue, 6 000 € of expenses and 3 600 € of RETA, without additional reliefs: rendimiento neto **48 400 €**, advance payments **9 680 €**, IRPF 2025 **12 538,05 € in Valencia** or **12 576,83 € in Asturias**.
- Modelo 720/721 are separate information obligations about foreign assets. They do not exempt the income from these assets from tax. The 50 000 € threshold is not a single threshold for "all foreign property".

## Rules

1. **Method and income.** In 2025–2026 EDS applies when the INCN of all actividades in the previous year is ≤600 000 €, if there is no renuncia and no incompatibilidad with estimación directa normal/objetiva. If the previous activity started during that year, the amount is annualised for the threshold when needed. Exceeding the limit or a waiver leads to normal for the prescribed three-year period. By default devengo applies, not the date the money arrives. The permitted cobros y pagos option applies to all actividades and binds you for at least three years. Ingresos include accrued services, applicable subvenciones, autoconsumo and other income. Revenue is not reduced by the retención. Ordinary IVA repercutido is not income. Deductible IVA soportado is not an expense. The sale of a fixed asset usually creates a separate ganancia/pérdida patrimonial, not activity revenue. Sources: [LIRPF arts. 14, 27–30](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764), [RIRPF arts. 7, 28–30](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-6820), [AEAT EDS](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/irpf/empresarios-individuales-profesionales/regimenes-determinar-rendimiento-actividad/estimacion-directa-simplificada.html).

2. **Expenses: link to income, document, recording.** In 2025–2026 the following are mandatory: correlación con ingresos/actividad, imputación temporal and entry in the libros registro. Usually a factura identifying the recipient is required. An electronic factura has the same value as a paper one. A bank statement alone does not replace a factura. However, RETA, salaries, taxes and bank fees are supported by the relevant document, not by a factura that does not exist. The need for an expense is not proven by the category name alone. Also keep contracts, the purpose of the trip or training, proof of payment and afectación. The full set of categories and casillas is given below. Sources: [AEAT gastos deducibles](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2025/7-cumplimentacion-irpf/7_4-rendimientos-actividades-economicas/7_4_2-regimen-estimacion-directa/7_4_2_3-gastos-fiscalmente-deducibles.html), [AEAT summary table](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/cuadro-resumen-determinacion-rendimiento-neto.html), [official Modelo 100 for 2025, anexo I](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2026/03/27/pdfs/BOE-A-2026-7041.pdf).

3. **Home, car and phone.** In 2025–2026, suministros of a partly afectada vivienda habitual: actual expense × area exclusivamente destinada a actividad / total area ×30%, unless a different share is proven. This is not 30% of the whole factura. It is not the rule for rent/IBI/amortización: these are allocated by the part that is really afectada, by ownership and by time of use. A separate professional office does not use the home formula. According to the AEAT position, a mobile line must be used exclusively for the actividad. An ordinary turismo with personal use does not become 50% deducible in IRPF: that presumption exists in IVA, not here. Exceptions for incidental private use exist, for example taxi, autoescuela, agentes comerciales, mixed goods transport and rental. They require facts of the relevant activity. Sources: [LIRPF art. 30.2.5ª](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a30), [AEAT suministros](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/gastos-fiscalmente-deducibles/servicios-exteriores/suministros.html), [AEAT afectación](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c06-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-cuestiones-generales/elementos-patrimoniales-afectos-actividad-economica/criterios-afectacion-bienes-derechos-ejercicio.html).

4. **Manutención, seguros and RETA.** The domestic meal rules apply from 01.01.2018. The limits below apply in 2025–2026. The following are required: your own expense in the course of the actividad, a restaurante/hostelería, electronic payment and a document. A working day alone does not prove the link. The reference to employee dietas transfers the amount limits, but not all conditions of the employee regime: you cannot automatically reject every lunch in your own city. The higher limit requires a pernocta in a municipio different from both the habitual lugar de trabajo and the residencia. A hotel is a separate documented expense. It is not part of the meal limit. Seguro de enfermedad: up to 500 €/year for the autónomo, the spouse and children under 25 living with them; 1 500 € for the relevant person with discapacidad. Ordinary RETA is deductible. An additional regularización payment is an expense of the year it is paid. A refund reduces the expense of the year of the refund. Any excess over the current cuotas is income. An old IRPF return is not corrected just because of the standard RETA regularización. Sources: [AEAT gastos titular, including the new RETA casillas 2025](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/gastos-fiscalmente-deducibles/gastos-titular-actividad.html), [LIRPF art. 30](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a30).

5. **Amortización instead of immediate expensing of capital purchases.** In 2025–2026 EDS uses the linear tabla simplificada for the relevant assets. The amortised value excludes recoverable IVA. It includes non-deductible IVA and direct installation costs. It excludes the value of land. Amortisation runs from the puesta en funcionamiento, in proportion to time, and not above the remaining base. New tangible items costing ≤300 €/unit allow libertad de amortización, with a total limit of 25 000 € per tax period. A short tax period reduces the limit. However, starting an actividad in the middle of a normal calendar year does not by itself make the tax period short. Sources: [AEAT tabla](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/folleto-actividades-economicas/3-impuesto-sobre-renta-personas-fisicas/3_5-estimacion-directa-simplificada/3_5_4-tabla-amortizacion-simplificada.html), [AEAT libertad art. 12.3 LIS](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/amortizaciones-dotaciones-ejercicio-fiscalmente-deducibles/supuestos-libertad-amortizacion/libertad-amortizacion-supuestos-contemplados-articulo-12_3.html).

6. **Gastos de difícil justificación.** In both years: 5% of the positive difference ingresos − other expenses, including amortización. The total annual maximum is 2 000 €. This is a single package of provisiones + difícil justificación. Additional ordinary provisiones are not added on top. With several actividades, the total limit is split between the positive rendimientos in proportion. This relief is incompatible with art. 32.2.1º, but compatible with art. 32.2.3º and inicio. The temporary 7% for 2023 cannot be carried over to 2025/2026. Sources: [RIRPF art. 30](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-6820#a30), [AEAT 2025 provisiones](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/gastos-fiscalmente-deducibles/provisiones.html).

7. **Art. 32.2, do not mix with art. 20.** From 01.01.2023 with no end date, including 2025–2026: art. 32.2.1º gives 2 000 € and, with low income, an additional amount of up to 6 498 €. All of these are needed at the same time: estimación directa; a único cliente no vinculado or a valid TRADE with a main client that is not related; expenses of all actividades ≤30% of ingresos; libros and the other formalities; no rendimientos del trabajo, except the listed social benefits totalling ≤4 000 €; ≥70% of the ingresos of the period sujetos a retención; no actividad through an entidad en atribución de rentas. Having one foreign client does not by itself create this right. If the conditions are not met but total rentas no exentas are <12 000 €, a separate reducción under art. 32.2.3º of up to 1 620 € is possible. The reducciones do not create a negative resultado. The art. 20 amounts for salaries, changed from 2024, do not replace art. 32. Sources: [LIRPF art. 32 in force](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a32), [AEAT requisitos](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-3-determinacion-rendimiento-neto-total/reducciones-generales-ejercicio/reduccion-trabajadores-autonomos/requisitos.html), [AEAT low total income](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-3-determinacion-rendimiento-neto-total/reducciones-generales-ejercicio/reduccion-contribuyentes-rentas-no-exentas.html).

8. **Inicio: art. 32.3.** For activities started from 01.01.2013, including those relevant to 2025/2026: 20% of the positive neto after the applicable art. 32.1/32.2. The base of the relief is at most 100 000 €/year. The periods are the first one with a positive rendimiento and the one immediately after it, not any two profitable years. In the year before the start there must be no other actividad. The exception is a ceased actividad that never gave a positive neto. It cannot be applied in a year in which >50% of ingresos came from a former employer from whom there were rendimientos del trabajo in the year before the start. Another nueva actividad does not restart the period if the first one continues. Sources: [LIRPF art. 32.3](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a32), [AEAT inicio](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-3-determinacion-rendimiento-neto-total/reduccion-rendimiento-neto-inicio-actividad-economica.html).

9. **Retenciones and Modelo 130.** In 2025–2026 a profesional receives gross income. The client withholds 15% or 7% of the fee without IVA. For 7%, there must be no professional activity in the year before the start. You must inform the payer of the circumstances, and the payer keeps the signed comunicación. This is the calendar year of the start + the next two, not 36 months. An ordinary extranjero without a Spanish EP/obligation to retener, and a private consumer, usually do not apply a Spanish retención. Not every actividad empresarial is subject to professional retención: the correct IAE/classification of the income is needed. The threshold for exemption from 130 applies to profesionales. In an ordinary continuing business the previous calendar year is assessed; at inicio, the quarter. Sources: [RIRPF arts. 76, 95, 109–110](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-6820), [AEAT instrucciones 130](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/impuestos-tasas/impuesto-sobre-renta-personas-fisicas/modelo-130-irpf______esionales-estimacion-directa-fraccionado_/instrucciones.html), [official form 130](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2015/02/19/pdfs/BOE-A-2015-1656.pdf).

10. **Annual IRPF and mínimos.** For 2025–2026 the estatal and the regional cuotas of the general base are added together. Ahorro is calculated separately. The estatal scale of arts. 63 applies from 2021. The last ahorro brackets were changed from 01.01.2025 to 30% in total. The mínimo personal/familiar is applied with the method "tax on the full base minus tax on the mínimo". If the general base is not enough, the rest of the mínimo goes to ahorro. Mínimos familiares require age, convivencia/dependencia, income of the person ≤8 000 € without exentas, and limits if that person files their own return >1 800 €. Splitting between relatives who have the right is mandatory. Sources: [LIRPF arts. 56–61, 63, 66, 74, 76](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764), [Valencia art. 2 bis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a2-2), [Asturias mínimos 2025](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c14-adecuacion-impuesto-circunstancias-personales/minimo-autonomico-personal-familiar/comunidad-autonoma-principado-asturias.html).

11. **Regional scales must be versioned.** Asturias Ley 3/2025 set a new scale and mínimos applying to ejercicio 2025. They remain in the checked wording for 2026. The Valencia scale for 2025 is the previous art. 2. Ley 5/2026 of 31 July, art. 18, changed it for devengos from 01.01.2026. DT3 already sets the next scale from 01.01.2027. Sources: [Asturias Ley 3/2025](https://www.boe.es/eli/es-as/l/2025/11/19/3), [Asturias texto refundido](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-945), [Valencia 2025 AEAT](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c15-calculo-impuesto-determinacion-cuotas-integras/gravamen-base-liquidable-general/gravamen-autonomico/comunitat-valenciana.html), [Valencia art. 2 in force and the 2027 note](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a2).

12. **Main regional deducciones for an individual taxpayer.** In 2025–2026: Valencia alquiler habitual 20%/800 €, 25%/950 € with one special condition, 30%/1 100 € with two. The special conditions are: ≤35 years, recognised discapacidad, víctima de violencia de género. The limit is reduced by the days of the contract/conditions, by the number of relevant filers and by income. It requires a suitable contract of at least one year after 23.04.1998, actual residence, no right to the incompatible deducción inversión vivienda, the restrictions on another available home <50 km away, and bank payment. Asturias: ordinary alquiler 10%/500 €, preferential 30%/1 500 € for young people, for the relevant rural concejos and for other listed groups. Inicio autónomo in a suitable concejo gives 1 000 €, but not for a simple move of an autónomo who is already active. Traslado por motivos laborales gives 15%/1 000 € (up to 2 000 € for trabajos especialmente cualificados). If the conditions are met, it is also available for cuenta propia. Gastos vitales for young people: up to 2 000/1 500/1 000 €. These are deductions from the regional cuota, not business expenses. Sources and full conditions: [Valencia alquiler](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunitat-valenciana/arrendamiento-pago-cesion-uso-vivienda-habitual.html), [Asturias alquiler](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2025/10-cumplimentacion-irpf-deducciones-autonomicas/10_3-principado-asturias/10_3_4-arrendamiento-vivienda-habitual.html), [Asturias inicio](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-principado-asturias/contribuyentes-que-se-establezcan-autonomos-despoblamiento.html), [Asturias arts. 7, 14 quinquies, 14 octies, 14 sexdecies](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-945), [gastos vitales AEAT](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-principado-asturias/gastos-vitales-que-incurran-contribuyentes-anos.html).

13. **Moving between autonomous communities.** In 2025–2026, art. 72: first, the region with the largest number of days in the tax period. Temporary absences are not excluded. The habitual vivienda is a rebuttable presumption. If the days cannot be determined, the principal centro de intereses applies, by the income categories listed in the article. After that, the last declared residencia. The location of a foreign client is not the centro de gestión of a Spanish autónomo. A move in March usually gives the destination region for the whole year N. A move in October usually gives the previous region for the whole of N, and the destination region for N+1 if residence there is permanent. You need a count of actual days, not a mechanical comparison with 183, especially with several regions. Anti-abuse: the aim of a lower IRPF can deprive the move of effect. There is a rebuttable presumption when all of these occur together: growth of the base imponible ≥50% in N or N+1 compared with N−1, a lower effective tax, and a return to the previous region in one of the two years after the growth year. The exception to the presumption is a continuous new residencia of ≥3 years. **There is no condition "the regional cuota fell by 50%".** Sources: [LIRPF art. 72](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a72), [AEAT criteria](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/Ayuda/25Presentacion/100/5_4.html).

14. **Modelo 720.** In 2025–2026 an ordinary resident checks three blocks separately: cuentas; valores/derechos/seguros/rentas; inmuebles/derechos. The initial obligation arises at >50 000 € per block. For accounts, it arises if the total saldo at 31.12 **or** the average saldo in Q4 exceeds the threshold. When the obligation arises, the whole relevant block is declared, not just the excess. After that: growth >20 000 € compared with the amount of the last mandatory return, or separate events of ending the status or disposing of assets that were previously declarable. Joint ownership does not mean comparing the threshold only with your own share: the full value is reported with the porcentaje de participación. An account on which the person is autorizado also needs a separate check. Sources: [AEAT FAQ 720](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-720-decla_____sobre-bienes-derechos-extranjero_/preguntas-frecuentes.html), [threshold](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-720-decla_____sobre-bienes-derechos-extranjero_/preguntas-frecuentes/forma-calcular-limite-que-obliga-declarar.html), [repeat filing](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-720-decla_____sobre-bienes-derechos-extranjero_/preguntas-frecuentes/frecuencia-presentacion-declaracion.html).

15. **Modelo 721.** From ejercicio 2023, including 2025–2026: cryptocurrencies held with a foreign key custodio that is not obliged to report in the corresponding way in Spain; a total of >50 000 € at 31.12. Your own non-custodial/cold wallet is not included just because the blockchain is international. Repeat filing: growth >20 000 € compared with the last return. The end of previously declared holding is reported even when the value falls below the threshold. A first purchase and sale in the same year before 31.12, without a previous obligation, does not by itself require a cancelación report. For full reinvestment into other foreign cryptocurrencies that are declared at 31.12, the FAQ allows reporting only the final balances. Fiat on an exchange does not become cryptocurrency for 721. Check 720 separately. Sources: [AEAT sujetos 721](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-721-decla-sobre-monedas-extranjero/preguntas-frecuentes-sobre-modelo-721.html), [situación extranjero](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-721-decla-sobre-monedas-extranjero/preguntas-frecuentes-sobre-modelo-721/cuando-se-entiende-moneda-virtual-extranjero.html), [threshold](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-721-decla-sobre-monedas-extranjero/preguntas-frecuentes-sobre-modelo-721/respecto-que-monedas-virtuales-no-informacion.html), [deadline/repeat events](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-721-decla-sobre-monedas-extranjero/preguntas-frecuentes-sobre-modelo-721/plazo-presentacion-modelo-721.html).

### Rendimiento neto and reducciones

```text
For each YTD period / year and each actividad:
  I = recognised ingresos, gross before retención, without ordinary IVA repercutido
  E = allowable current expenses + RETA + amortización
  P = I - E

If art.32.2.1º is chosen in the annual calculation:
  GDJ = 0
Otherwise, for all EDS actividades of the taxpayer:
  positive_total = sum(max(P_activity, 0))
  GDJ_total = R(min(2000, 0.05 * positive_total))
  split GDJ_total in proportion to the positive P;
  give 0 to negative P; split the remaining cents so that the total is kept.
  For one negocio: GDJ = R(min(2000, 0.05 * max(P, 0)))
  In Modelo130 cap=2000 for each YTD calculation, not 500 per quarter;
  this is one annual ceiling in the cumulative base, not 2000 of new expense each quarter.

RN = P - GDJ
R32_1 = 30% of the qualifying irregular neto, base at most 300000/year,
        only art.32.1, not ordinary recurring monthly income.
RN_reducido = RN - R32_1

If ALL conditions of art.32.2.1º/2º are met:
  threshold_RN = total neto of actividades before reducciones, without GDJ
  low_addition = 0
  if other_nonexempt_income <= 6500 and threshold_RN < 19747.50:
    if threshold_RN <= 14047.50: low_addition = 6498
    otherwise: low_addition = R(6498 - 1.14*(threshold_RN-14047.50))
  disability_addition = 0 /3500 /7750 according to the conditions
  R32_2_1 = min(max(sum(RN_reducido),0), 2000+low_addition+disability_addition)
  R32_2_3 = 0
Otherwise:
  R32_2_1 = 0
  T = algebraic sum of all rentas no exentas under the AEAT rule,
      neto before reducciones, without the integración/compensación rules;
      for trabajo the preceding gasto reducción art.18 is allowed.
  candidate = 1620 if T<=8000;
              R(1620 - 0.405*(T-8000)) if 8000<T<12000;
              0 otherwise
  R32_2_3 = min(candidate, max(sum(RN_reducido),0), max(3700-R_art20,0))

after32_2 = sum(RN_reducido)-R32_2_1-R32_2_3
R_inicio = R(0.20*min(max(after32_2,0),100000)) with a right under art.32.3,
           otherwise 0
rendimiento_neto_reducido_total = after32_2-R_inicio
```

For art.32.2.1º and GDJ, calculate the lawful alternatives without applying both at once. Store the choice explicitly. In Modelo 130, art.32.2 and the reducción inicio do not reduce casilla03: the annual IRPF applies them. Art.32.1, on the contrary, is taken into account directly in casilla03 according to the instrucciones. The special reducción artistas DA60 does not apply to an ordinary consultant/developer. If the IAE is artistic, a separate branch is required. Do not automatically assume 0237=0.

Mapping 100: `0180=I`, `0218=E`, `0221=I−E`, `0222=GDJ`, `0223=E+GDJ`, `0224=RN`, `0225=R32_1`, `0226=RN−R32_1` when there is no special 0236; `0231=sum(0226)`, `0232=R32_2_1`, `0233=R32_2_3`, `0234=R_inicio`, `0235=0231−0232−0233−0234` for a profile without 0237.

### Amortización

```text
cost_basis = precio + gastos_directos + IVA_no_deducible - valor_suelo
remaining = cost_basis - amortización_fiscal_ya_deducida
annual_rate = chosen allowable linear rate from the tabla
period_amort = min(remaining, R(cost_basis*annual_rate*days_in_use/days_in_year))
```

The rate does not have to be the máximo. Under the ordinary linear table the lower rate is set by the maximum period. For the annual/YTD calculation take the accumulated amortisation. Do not again take the remaining value ×rate (that is a wrong declining-balance scheme). Buying a laptop for 2 000 € without deductible IVA, with a full year and 26%, gives 520 €, not 2 000 €. On sale/disposal, stop the amortisation and separately calculate the fiscal valor for the patrimonial resultado. Free/accelerated write-off is an explicit legal choice with the basis recorded. It is not a change of the ordinary table.

### Retención on an outgoing factura

```text
base_honorarios = taxable professional remuneration excluding IVA and true suplidos
rate = 0, if the pagador is not obliged to retener in Spain;
       0.07, if profesional inicio + notice + the conditions are met;
       0.15 in the ordinary professional B2B case
retencion = R(base_honorarios*rate)
importe_a_cobrar = honorarios + IVA_repercutido - retencion
ingreso_IRPF = honorarios, not importe_a_cobrar
```

Do not automatically record foreign withholding tax in 130[06]/100[0599] as a Spanish retención. Check separately the convenio, the right to a refund abroad and the deducción doble imposición art.80 in 100[0588].


### 720/721 checks for the planner

```text
For each block720:
  initial = no_previous_required_return_for_block and
            (block_value>50000 or for accounts quarter_average_total>50000)
  growth = relevant total indicator - last_required_reported_indicator >20000
  termination = end of a previously reportable right/status
  required = initial or growth or termination, with the applicable legal exceptions

For721:
  value31 = sum(custodied_abroad_units*documented_EUR_market_quote_at_31_Dec)
  initial = no_previous_required_return and value31>50000
  growth = value31-last_required_declared_value31>20000
  termination = extinguished_previously_reportable_holdings,
                with the exception for permitted full reinversión from the FAQ
```

You need the history of returns, the full value/share/role, the custodian and country, the date of acquisition/end, the EUR valuation and the source of the quote/exchange rate. For 720 cuentas, both indicators are kept (31.12 and the Q4 average). Securities are valued under the applicable Patrimonio rules. Real estate is valued at valor de adquisición with the attributable costs and taxes. Insurance is valued at valor rescate or the prescribed alternative. Currency revaluation can affect the threshold for accounts/securities. Do not replace the statutory value of foreign real estate with an annual market price. These returns are informational. Their totals are not automatically transferred to taxable income in 100.

## Edge cases

1. **One foreign client ≠ the TRADE relief.** These are separate: the civil/labour classification as TRADE (usually ≥75% dependencia and the other requirements), art.32.2 (including 70% sujetos a retención) and the 130 exemption (70% of income actually subject to retención in the relevant reference period). The first does not automatically meet the other two.
2. **Start in December.** The 7% covers December and the next two calendar years. Inicio 20% starts from the first positive year, even if it lasted one month. A following loss-making year does not push the "second year of the relief" further.
3. **Factura diciembre, payment febrero.** Under devengo, include the income in the December year. The retención arises under the payment rules. The allocation of the annual credit is reconciled with the imputación of the relevant income and the certificado. Under a lawful cobros y pagos the result is different. A change of criterion must not lose or duplicate income.
4. **Own meals in your own city.** Not an automatic ban and not an automatic permission. Prove the actividad, the restaurante and electronic payment. An ordinary personal daily lunch does not become an expense because of autónomo status. The higher overnight cap requires a different municipio.
5. **Home office.** Keep the area and the date of afectación, the change after a move, and the title documents. 20 m² out of 100 m² → suministros 6%, but documented rent of the afectada part is usually 20%, not 6%. A shared room that is also used personally is not accepted as an office just by an arbitrary percentage.
6. **Car and mixed-use smartphone.** Do not inherit the IVA coefficient. For an ordinary turismo, prove exclusivo negocio. For the móvil, a separate business line is convenient. An arbitrary 50% is not a safe AEAT rule.
7. **RETA regularización and SL administrator.** A single cuota cannot be deducted twice, as gasto actividad and as the administrator's trabajo. AEAT allows choosing one of the relevant categories when both reasons for cotización exist. Prestación incapacidad temporal is usually trabajo, not a factura negocio. It can break the conditions of 32.2.
8. **Part of a year under Beckham, exit/exclusion.** This document does not allow a month-by-month mix of 210/151 and 100. First establish the regime for the whole period under the rules of 93. Only then use ordinary IRPF. A move between regions does not by itself end Beckham and does not move it to the regional scale.
9. **Move in N, rent in two regions.** One tax residence for N. Regional expenses are checked under the rules of the chosen region. The address on 31 December does not replace the days. You need to monitor a return to the old region in later years for 72.3.
10. **Asturias rural.** Continuing a previous actividad after an ordinary change of address is not a new alta with the automatic 1 000 € relief. Not every village is included in Decreto 83/2025. For traslado, remote work does not automatically prove a motivo laboral for the move.
11. **Foreign assets and income.** Income from a Russian account/broker is taxed under ordinary IRPF, even if the account is <50 000 € and 720 is not required. Sanctions or difficulty transferring money are not a tax exemption. Check the possibility of double taxation separately under the current Spain–Russia relations.
12. **Loss/high retenciones.** [07] of 130 can become negative. [12] is limited to 0, but an unused [13] can produce a negative [19]. The calculation must distinguish these two negative values. An overpaid advance payment is refunded through 100.
13. **Retención not paid by the client.** The supplier has a special right to account for the legal retención in the circumstances of art.99.5 LIRPF. However, you cannot automatically treat the tax as withheld without a document and an analysis of the reason. Store separately: gross, actually withheld, withholding under the law and the certificado.
14. **Mínimos and regional reliefs.** The state mínimo and the regional mínimo can differ. Carry the remainder to ahorro with two independent calculations. A regional deducción without an expressly provided refundable regime does not create a negative cuota regional.
15. **Previous forms and corrections.** Do not add the original and the corrected 130 as two advance payments. For 100 for 2025, the autoliquidación rectificativa applies. HTML help about old complementarias may not reflect the new procedure. The tax logic and the correction mechanism must be versioned separately.

## Open questions

- **Two identified errors/contradictions in the HTML instruction for 130.** The text of casilla 19 says to subtract 17 from 18, but the approved printed form clearly sets **17−18**. In the same place, for apartadoI, "660,14 anuales" is inserted after "660,14 por trimestre". The current **RIRPF110.3.d.1º** and the form confirm the quarterly limit for estimación directa. The annual limit belongs to a different branch. The engine must use the law/form. The formulas kept above already take this discrepancy into account.
- **2026 Modelo 100: numbers and format UNVERIFIED.** As of the control date, the sources examined contain no confirmed final form for ejercicio 2026/campaign 2027. The legal rates for 2026 are confirmed, but the 2026 export cannot be declared ready only on the basis of the 2025 mapping. The 130 instruction mentions last year's casillas 2021–2026, but this does not replace publication of Modelo 100 2026 itself.
- **Tie-case rounding: UNVERIFIED as the exact Renta WEB implementation.** The sources require two decimal places, but do not disclose the algorithm for all borderline fractions of a cent. Here a reasonable Decimal half-up is set explicitly. Before production export, compare cases at the cent boundary with the official calculation. The numerical example does not depend on the tie-case.
- **Other activos/intangibles and amortización incentives.** The basic table and libertad ≤300 are confirmed. Special accelerated amortización ERD, nuevos vehículos/energía, assets from old years and special intangibles require a separate basis. Do not replace any regime with the figure 26%. Do not promise that one table covers every specific purchase.
- **720/721 exceptions.** Exceptions exist for assets that are properly individualised in prescribed accounting, and for other categories of obliged persons. Do not treat ordinary EDS libros as automatically sufficient contabilidad para exención. A specific broker/fintech/custodio, life insurance without rescate, a trust/beneficiary and reinvestment sequences need classification under the RGAT/FAQ. With unknown data the status is `requires_review`, not "no need to file".
- **Compatibility of a regional personal deduction with a gasto negocio.** No universal rule has been established that allows double use of one invoice for each regional relief. For gastos vitales/rent of a work office, separate the personal and the business part and check the special regime before automatic application. Mark disputed compatibility as **UNVERIFIED**. Do not present it as guaranteed savings.
- **Asturias age 30 in art.14 sexdecies.** The consolidated literal text contains an overlap of "26–30" and "30–35", but the AEAT Manual 2025 expressly interprets the second group as **31–35**, with the higher cap when a person falls into two groups in the year. The specification uses the AEAT clarification and keeps the reference for explainability.
- **Legislative changes after 08.10.2026.** 2026 is not closed yet: new or retroactive changes are possible. The currently confirmed 2027 delta in this area is the Valencia scale. It does not mean "all 2027 taxes are known". Neither the 2024 increase of the salary relief, nor the old 2024 rates, nor the current Valencia 2026 scale may overwrite the 2025 calculation.
- **A limitation of the example, not a hidden assumption.** Without age, housing/relocation expenses, other income, the start of the actividad and the split of transactions by quarter, there is no single numerical answer. For the required example all missing parameters are set explicitly. No builds/tests were run. The example was calculated arithmetically. It was not checked by filing in Renta WEB. Before implementation, the main agent must check the control example, the tramo boundaries and negative-carry 130 against the official service.
