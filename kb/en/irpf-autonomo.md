## Summary

- Scope: an individual who is tax resident in Spain, **ordinary IRPF**, economic activity under simplified direct estimation (EDS). This is not the calculation of the régimen Beckham. It is not the Corporate Income Tax (Impuesto sobre Sociedades) of an SL. Russian nationality alone does not change the rules. Worldwide income is taken into account.
- Checked against primary sources as of **08.10.2026**: the AEAT Manual Renta 2025, the LIRPF/RIRPF in force, and the laws of the Comunitat Valenciana and of Asturias. The 2026 legislation can already be used for the calculation. The final form and casillas of the Modelo 100 for 2026 cannot yet be considered approved.
- EDS: the turnover of all activities in the previous year must not exceed **600 000 €**. There must be no incompatible method and no waiver. Income minus documented expenses, RETA and depreciation. Then **5% for expenses that are hard to justify**, with a maximum of **2 000 € per year per taxpayer**, in 2025 and 2026.
- Important correction to the initial assumptions: own meals in Spain are **26,67 €/day without the corresponding overnight stay and 53,34 €/day with it**, not 48,08 €. Abroad, 48,08/91,35 €. This is a cap on the real expense. It is not an automatic daily allowance.
- Art. 32.2 for certain autónomos is not a general benefit for any freelancer. Besides a single client/TRADE, it requires that ≥70% of income is subject to withholding, plus other conditions. **6 498 €**, not 7 302 €, is the additional reduction for low income. 7 302 € belongs to employment income under art. 20.
- Withholding for a professional: normally **15%**. For a new professional who meets the conditions, **7% in the start year and in the next two calendar tax years**. Normally the Spanish B2B client withholds. A foreign client without a Spanish obligation to withhold does not.
- The Modelo 130 is an instalment payment. It is not the final 20% tax. The data are cumulative from 1 January. Casilla 05 is not simply the money actually paid before. The form defines it like this: «From the previous quarters: sum of the positive amounts of casilla 07 minus the sum of the amounts of casilla 16».
- A professional activity is exempt from the 130 if in the previous year ≥70% of its income was subject to withholding. In the first year, the relevant quarter is checked. This does not exempt from the annual Modelo 100.
- The personal minimum reduces the **tax amount per the scale**. It does not reduce the base before the marginal rate is applied. In the example, under 65 years: state 5 550 €, regional in the two regions considered 6 105 €.
- **Asturias changed its scale and minimums already for 2025**: you cannot use the old minimum of 5 550 € or the old starting rate of 10%. **Valencia changed its scale for 2026**, and a further cut for **2027 is already approved**, Ley 5/2026.
- A move does not split the annual IRPF into months of two regions. A single Autonomous Community is determined, mainly by the largest number of days in the year. Then its scale and its minimums apply to the whole year.
- With 60 000 € of income, 6 000 € of expenses and 3 600 € of RETA, without additional benefits: net income **48 400 €**, instalment payments **9 680 €**, IRPF 2025 **12 538,05 € in Valencia** or **12 576,83 € in Asturias**.
- Modelos 720/721 are separate information obligations about assets abroad. They are not an exemption from tax on the income from those assets. The 50 000 € threshold is not a single threshold for «all assets abroad».

## Rules

1. **Method and income.** In 2025–2026 EDS applies when the INCN of all activities in the previous year is ≤600 000 €, if there is no waiver and no incompatibility with normal direct estimation/objective estimation. If needed, the previous activity's start year is annualised for the threshold. Exceeding the threshold or a waiver means the normal mode for the planned three-year period. By default the accrual basis applies, not the payment date. The permitted cash basis (cobros y pagos) option applies to all activities and binds for at least three years. Income includes accrued services, applicable subsidies, own consumption and other income. Income is not reduced by the withholding. Ordinary output IVA (IVA repercutido) is not income. Deductible input IVA (IVA soportado) is not an expense. The sale of a fixed asset normally gives a separate capital gain/loss. It is not activity income. Sources: [LIRPF arts. 14, 27–30](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764), [RIRPF arts. 7, 28–30](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-6820), [AEAT EDS](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/irpf/empresarios-individuales-profesionales/regimenes-determinar-rendimiento-actividad/estimacion-directa-simplificada.html).

2. **Expenses: link to income, document, record.** In 2025–2026 the following are mandatory: the link to income/activity, the time allocation, and the entry in the record books. Normally an invoice with the identification of the recipient is required. An electronic invoice is equal to a paper one. A bank statement alone does not replace the invoice. However, RETA, salaries, taxes and bank fees are supported by the relevant document, not by an invoice that does not exist. The need for an expense is not proved only by the category name. Also keep the contracts, the purpose of the trip/training, the proof of payment and the business use (afectación). The full set of categories and casillas is below. Sources: [AEAT deductible expenses](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2025/7-cumplimentacion-irpf/7_4-rendimientos-actividades-economicas/7_4_2-regimen-estimacion-directa/7_4_2_3-gastos-fiscalmente-deducibles.html), [AEAT summary table](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/cuadro-resumen-determinacion-rendimiento-neto.html), [official Modelo 100 for 2025, annex I](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2026/03/27/pdfs/BOE-A-2026-7041.pdf).

3. **Home, vehicle and phone.** In 2025–2026, utilities of a main home that is partly used for the business: real expense × area used exclusively for the activity / total area × 30%, unless another proportion is proved. It is not 30% of the whole bill. It is also not the rule for rent/IBI/depreciation: these are split by the part really used for the business, the ownership and the time of use. A separate professional office does not follow the home formula. According to the AEAT criterion, the mobile line must be used exclusively for the activity. An ordinary passenger car with personal use does not become 50% deductible in IRPF. That presumption exists in IVA, not here. There are exceptions for incidental private use, for example: taxi, driving school, sales agents, mixed goods transport and rental. You need facts that prove the corresponding activity. Sources: [LIRPF art. 30.2.5ª](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a30), [AEAT utilities](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/gastos-fiscalmente-deducibles/servicios-exteriores/suministros.html), [AEAT business use](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c06-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-cuestiones-generales/elementos-patrimoniales-afectos-actividad-economica/criterios-afectacion-bienes-derechos-ejercicio.html).

4. **Meals, insurance and RETA.** The rules on meals in Spain apply from 01.01.2018. The limits below apply in 2025–2026. An own expense during the activity is required, in a restaurant/hospitality business, with electronic payment and a document. A working day alone does not prove the link to the activity. The reference to employee allowances (dietas) carries over the amount limits. It does not carry over all the conditions of the employee regime. You cannot automatically reject every meal in your own city. The higher limit requires an overnight stay in a municipality different from both the usual workplace and the residence. The hotel is a separate documented expense. It is not part of the meals limit. Health insurance: up to 500 €/year for the autónomo, for the spouse and for children under 25 who live with them. 1 500 € for each person with the relevant disability. Ordinary RETA is deductible. An additional payment from a regularisation is an expense of the year in which it is paid. A refund reduces the expense of the year in which it is refunded. The excess over the current contributions is income. The IRPF of earlier years is not corrected just because of an ordinary RETA regularisation. Sources: [AEAT owner's expenses, including the new RETA 2025 casillas](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/gastos-fiscalmente-deducibles/gastos-titular-actividad.html), [LIRPF art. 30](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a30).

5. **Depreciation instead of an immediate deduction of investment purchases.** In 2025–2026 EDS uses the simplified straight-line table for the relevant assets. The value is depreciated without the recoverable IVA, with the non-deductible IVA and the direct set-up costs, without the value of the land. Depreciation starts when the asset is put into use, in proportion to time, and does not exceed the remaining base. New tangible items with a value ≤300 €/unit allow free depreciation (libertad de amortización), with a total limit of 25 000 €/tax period. A short tax period reduces the limit. Starting the activity in the middle of an ordinary calendar year does not by itself make the tax period short. Sources: [AEAT table](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/folleto-actividades-economicas/3-impuesto-sobre-renta-personas-fisicas/3_5-estimacion-directa-simplificada/3_5_4-tabla-amortizacion-simplificada.html), [AEAT free depreciation art. 12.3 LIS](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/amortizaciones-dotaciones-ejercicio-fiscalmente-deducibles/supuestos-libertad-amortizacion/libertad-amortizacion-supuestos-contemplados-articulo-12_3.html).

6. **Expenses that are hard to justify.** In both years: 5% of the positive difference between income and the other expenses, including depreciation. Total annual maximum of 2 000 €. It is a single package of provisions + hard-to-justify expenses. No other ordinary provisions are added on top. With several activities, the total limit is split proportionally among the positive results. The benefit is incompatible with art. 32.2.1º. It is compatible with art. 32.2.3º and with the start-up reduction. The temporary 7% of 2023 cannot be carried over to 2025/2026. Sources: [RIRPF art. 30](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-6820#a30), [AEAT 2025 provisions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/gastos-fiscalmente-deducibles/provisiones.html).

7. **Art. 32.2: do not confuse it with art. 20.** From 01.01.2023, with no time limit, including 2025–2026: art. 32.2.1º gives 2 000 € and, with low income, an additional amount of up to 6 498 €. All of the following are required at the same time: direct estimation; a single unrelated client or a valid TRADE with an unrelated main client; expenses of all activities ≤30% of income; books and the other formalities; no employment income, except the listed social benefits with a total ≤4 000 €; ≥70% of the period's income subject to withholding; no activity through an entity under the income attribution regime (atribución de rentas). Having a single foreign client alone does not give this right. If the conditions are not met, but total non-exempt income is <12 000 €, the separate reduction of art. 32.2.3º of up to 1 620 € is possible. The reductions do not create a negative result. The art. 20 amounts for salaries, changed from 2024, do not replace art. 32. Sources: [LIRPF in force art. 32](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a32), [AEAT requirements](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-3-determinacion-rendimiento-neto-total/reducciones-generales-ejercicio/reduccion-trabajadores-autonomos/requisitos.html), [AEAT low general income](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-3-determinacion-rendimiento-neto-total/reducciones-generales-ejercicio/reduccion-contribuyentes-rentas-no-exentas.html).

8. **Start-up: art. 32.3.** For activities started from 01.01.2013, including the cases relevant for 2025/2026: 20% of the positive net income after applying art. 32.1/32.2 as relevant. The base of the benefit is at most 100 000 €/year. The periods are the first one with a positive result and the one right after it. They are not any two profitable years. In the year before the start there must be no other activity. The exception is a ceased activity that never gave a positive net income. It cannot be applied in a year in which >50% of income comes from the former employer from whom employment income was received in the year before the start. Another new activity does not restart the period if the first one continues. Sources: [LIRPF art. 32.3](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a32), [AEAT start-up](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-3-determinacion-rendimiento-neto-total/reduccion-rendimiento-neto-inicio-actividad-economica.html).

9. **Withholding and Modelo 130.** In 2025–2026 the professional earns the gross income. The client withholds 15% or 7% of the fee without IVA. For the 7%, there must be no professional activity in the year before the start. The professional must tell the payer about this. The payer keeps the signed notice. It is the calendar start year + the next two, not 36 months. An ordinary foreign client without a PE in Spain/obligation to withhold and a private consumer normally do not apply Spanish withholding. Not every business activity is subject to professional withholding. You need the correct IAE heading (epígrafe)/the correct classification of the income. The 130 exemption threshold refers to professionals. For an ordinary continuing business, the previous calendar year is assessed. At the start, the quarter is assessed. Sources: [RIRPF arts. 76, 95, 109–110](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-6820), [AEAT 130 instructions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/impuestos-tasas/impuesto-sobre-renta-personas-fisicas/modelo-130-irpf______esionales-estimacion-directa-fraccionado_/instrucciones.html), [official 130 form](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2015/02/19/pdfs/BOE-A-2015-1656.pdf).

10. **Annual IRPF and minimums.** In 2025–2026 the state tax amount and the regional tax amount on the general base are added. Savings are calculated separately. The state scale of arts. 63 applies from 2021. The top savings brackets were changed from 01.01.2025 to 30% in total. The personal/family minimum is applied with the method «tax on the full base minus tax on the minimum». If the general base is not enough, the rest of the minimum moves to savings. Family minimums require age, living together/dependency and income of the person ≤8 000 € without counting exempt income. There are limits if that person files their own return with >1 800 €. Splitting between the family members with the right is mandatory. Sources: [LIRPF arts. 56–61, 63, 66, 74, 76](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764), [Valencia art. 2 bis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a2-2), [Asturias minimums 2025](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c14-adecuacion-impuesto-circunstancias-personales/minimo-autonomico-personal-familiar/comunidad-autonoma-principado-asturias.html).

11. **Regional scales must be versioned.** Ley 3/2025 of Asturias set a new scale and new minimums that apply to tax year 2025. They remain in the wording checked for 2026. The Valencia scale for 2025 is the earlier art. 2. Ley 5/2026, of 31 July, in its art. 18, changed it for accruals from 01.01.2026. DT3 already sets the next scale from 01.01.2027. Sources: [Asturias Ley 3/2025](https://www.boe.es/eli/es-as/l/2025/11/19/3), [Asturias consolidated text](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-945), [Valencia 2025 AEAT](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c15-calculo-impuesto-determinacion-cuotas-integras/gravamen-base-liquidable-general/gravamen-autonomico/comunitat-valenciana.html), [Valencia art. 2 in force and note on 2027](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1998-8202#a2).

12. **Main regional deductions for the individual taxpayer.** In 2025–2026: Valencia, rent of the main home 20%/800 €; 25%/950 € with one special condition; 30%/1 100 € with two. The special conditions are: ≤35 years, recognised disability, victim of gender violence. The limit is reduced by the days of the contract/of the conditions, by the number of filers with the right and by income. The following are required: a suitable contract of at least one year after 23.04.1998, actual residence, no right to the incompatible deduction for home investment, the limits on having another home at <50 km, and bank payment. Asturias: general rent 10%/500 €; subsidised rent 30%/1 500 € for young people/the relevant rural concejos and other listed groups. Starting as an autónomo in a suitable concejo: 1 000 €, but not for the mere move of an autónomo who already works. Move for work reasons: 15%/1 000 € (up to 2 000 € for especially qualified jobs). If the conditions are met, it is also available for self-employment. Living expenses for young people: up to 2 000/1 500/1 000 €. These are deductions from the regional tax amount. They are not business expenses. Sources and full conditions: [Valencia rent](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunitat-valenciana/arrendamiento-pago-cesion-uso-vivienda-habitual.html), [Asturias rent](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-ayuda-presentacion/irpf-2025/10-cumplimentacion-irpf-deducciones-autonomicas/10_3-principado-asturias/10_3_4-arrendamiento-vivienda-habitual.html), [Asturias start-up](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-principado-asturias/contribuyentes-que-se-establezcan-autonomos-despoblamiento.html), [Asturias arts. 7, 14 quinquies, 14 octies, 14 sexdecies](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-945), [AEAT living expenses](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025-deducciones-autonomicas/comunidad-autonoma-principado-asturias/gastos-vitales-que-incurran-contribuyentes-anos.html).

13. **Move between autonomous communities.** In 2025–2026, art. 72: first, the region with the largest number of days in the tax period. Temporary absences are not subtracted. The main home is a presumption that can be rebutted. If the days cannot be determined: the main centre of interests by the income categories listed in the article; then, the last declared residence. The location of a foreign client is not the management centre of the Spanish autónomo. A move in March normally gives the destination region for the whole year N. A move in October normally gives the previous region for the whole year N and the destination region for N+1 if the residence is permanent. Count the real days. Do not compare mechanically with 183, especially when there are several regions. Anti-abuse: the aim of paying less IRPF can make the move ineffective. There is a rebuttable presumption when all of the following occur together: an increase of the taxable base ≥50% in N or N+1 compared with N−1, lower effective taxation, and a return to the previous region in one of the two years after the year of the increase. Exception to the presumption: a new continuous residence ≥3 years. **The condition «the regional tax amount fell by 50%» does not exist.** Sources: [LIRPF art. 72](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764#a72), [AEAT criteria](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/Ayuda/25Presentacion/100/5_4.html).

14. **Modelo 720.** In 2025–2026 an ordinary resident checks three blocks separately: accounts; securities/rights/insurance/annuities; real estate/rights. The initial obligation arises with >50 000 € per block. For accounts, it arises if the joint balance at 31.12 **or** the average balance of Q4 exceeds the threshold. When the obligation arises, the whole relevant block is declared, not only the excess. After that: an increase >20 000 € compared with the value of the last mandatory return, or specific events of loss of the status/transfer of assets that were subject to declaration before. Co-ownership does not mean comparing the threshold only with your own share. The total value is reported with the ownership percentage. An account in which the person is an authorised user also needs a separate check. Sources: [AEAT FAQ 720](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-720-decla_____sobre-bienes-derechos-extranjero_/preguntas-frecuentes.html), [threshold](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-720-decla_____sobre-bienes-derechos-extranjero_/preguntas-frecuentes/forma-calcular-limite-que-obliga-declarar.html), [filing in later years](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-720-decla_____sobre-bienes-derechos-extranjero_/preguntas-frecuentes/frecuencia-presentacion-declaracion.html).

15. **Modelo 721.** From tax year 2023, including 2025–2026: virtual currencies held by a foreign key custodian that is not obliged to report in an equivalent way in Spain; in total >50 000 € at 31.12. An own non-custodial wallet/cold wallet is not included just because the blockchain is international. New return: an increase >20 000 € compared with the last return. The end of a holding declared before is also reported when the value falls below the threshold. Buying and selling for the first time in the same year before 31.12, with no earlier obligation, does not by itself require reporting the cancellation. In case of full reinvestment in other virtual currencies abroad that are declared at 31.12, the FAQ allow reporting only the final balances. Fiat money on an exchange does not become a virtual currency for the purposes of the 721. The 720 must be checked separately. Sources: [AEAT 721 obliged persons](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-721-decla-sobre-monedas-extranjero/preguntas-frecuentes-sobre-modelo-721.html), [situation abroad](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-721-decla-sobre-monedas-extranjero/preguntas-frecuentes-sobre-modelo-721/cuando-se-entiende-moneda-virtual-extranjero.html), [threshold](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-721-decla-sobre-monedas-extranjero/preguntas-frecuentes-sobre-modelo-721/respecto-que-monedas-virtuales-no-informacion.html), [deadline/later events](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-721-decla-sobre-monedas-extranjero/preguntas-frecuentes-sobre-modelo-721/plazo-presentacion-modelo-721.html).

```text

  I = recognised income, gross before withholding, without ordinary output IVA
  E = eligible current expenses + RETA + depreciation
  P = I - E

If art.32.2.1º is chosen in the annual calculation:
  GDJ = 0
Otherwise, for all EDS activities of the taxpayer:
  positive_total = sum(max(P_activity, 0))
  GDJ_total = R(min(2000, 0.05 * positive_total))
  split GDJ_total in proportion to the positive P values;
  assign 0 to negative P values; split the leftover cents so that the sum is kept.
  For a single business: GDJ = R(min(2000, 0.05 * max(P, 0)))
  In Modelo130, cap=2000 for each YTD calculation, not 500 per quarter;
  it is one annual cap on the cumulative base, not 2000 of new expense each quarter.

RN = P - GDJ
R32_1 = 30% of the qualifying irregular net income, maximum base 300000/year,
        art.32.1 only, not ordinary recurring monthly income.
RN_reducido = RN - R32_1

If ALL the conditions of art.32.2.1º/2º are met:
  threshold_RN = total net income of the activities before reductions, without GDJ

  if other_nonexempt_income <= 6500 and threshold_RN < 19747.50:

  R32_2_1 = min(max(sum(RN_reducido),0), 2000+low_addition+disability_addition)
  R32_2_3 = 0
Otherwise:
  R32_2_1 = 0
```

### Depreciation

```text
cost_basis = precio + gastos_directos + IVA_no_deducible - valor_suelo
remaining = cost_basis - amortización_fiscal_ya_deducida
annual_rate = eligible straight-line rate chosen from the table
period_amort = min(remaining, R(cost_basis*annual_rate*days_in_use/days_in_year))
```

The rate does not have to be the maximum. With the ordinary straight-line table, the minimum is determined by the maximum period. For the annual/YTD calculation, take the cumulative depreciation. Do not multiply the remaining value × rate again (that would be a wrong declining-balance scheme). Buying a laptop for 2 000 € without deductible IVA, with the full year and 26%, gives 520 €, not 2 000 €. When the item is sold/removed, stop the depreciation and calculate the tax value for the capital result separately. Free depreciation/accelerated depreciation is an explicit legal option with a record of its basis. It is not a change of the ordinary table.

### 720/721 checks for the planner

```text
For each block720:
  initial = no_previous_required_return_for_block and
            (block_value>50000 or, for accounts, quarter_average_total>50000)
  growth = relevant total indicator - last_required_reported_indicator >20000
  termination = end of a right/status that had to be reported before
  required = initial or growth or termination, with the applicable legal exceptions

For721:
  value31 = sum(custodied_abroad_units*documented_EUR_market_quote_at_31_Dec)
  initial = no_previous_required_return and value31>50000
  growth = value31-last_required_declared_value31>20000
  termination = extinguished_previously_reportable_holdings,
                with the exception of eligible full reinvestment under the FAQ
```

You need the history of returns, the total value/the share/the role of the filer, the custodian and the country, the date of acquisition/extinction, the valuation in EUR, and the source of the quote/the exchange rate. For 720 accounts, both indicators are stored (31.12 and the Q4 average balance). Securities are valued under the applicable rules of the Wealth Tax (Impuesto sobre el Patrimonio). Real estate is valued at the acquisition value with the attributable costs and taxes. Insurance is valued at the surrender value/the planned alternative. Exchange rate changes can affect the threshold for accounts/securities. Do not replace the regulatory value of foreign real estate with an annual market price. The returns are informative. Their total is not automatically carried to the taxable income of the 100.

## Edge cases

1. **A single foreign client ≠ TRADE benefit.** These are separate things: the civil/labour classification as TRADE (normally ≥75% dependency and the other requirements), art. 32.2 (including the 70% subject to withholding), and the 130 exemption (70% of income that actually bore withholding in the relevant reference period). The first does not automatically meet the other two conditions.
2. **Start in December.** The 7% covers December and the next two calendar years. The 20% start-up reduction begins in the first positive year, even if it lasted one month. A following year with losses does not push the «second year of the benefit» further forward.
3. **Invoice in December, payment in February.** On the accrual basis, the income is included in the December year. The withholding arises under the payment rules. The allocation of the annual credit is reconciled with the allocation of the corresponding income and with the certificate. With the legal cash basis (cobros y pagos), the result is different. A change of criterion must not lose or duplicate income.
4. **Own meal in your own city.** It is neither an automatic ban nor an automatic permission. You must prove the activity, the restaurant and the electronic payment. An ordinary personal daily meal does not become an expense because you are an autónomo. The higher limit with an overnight stay requires another municipality.
5. **Office at home.** Keep the area and the date of business use, the change after a move, and the documents that prove the title. 20 m² out of 100 m² → utilities 6%; but the documented rent of the business part is normally 20%, not 6%. A shared space with simultaneous personal use is not recognised as an office just by an arbitrary percentage.
6. **Car and mixed-use smartphone.** Do not inherit the IVA rate. For an ordinary passenger car, prove exclusive business use. For the mobile, it is advisable to have a separate professional line. An arbitrary 50% is not a safe AEAT rule.
7. **RETA regularisation and SL director.** A single contribution cannot be deducted twice, as an activity expense and as an employment expense of the director. The AEAT allows choosing one of the relevant categories when both grounds for contribution apply. The temporary incapacity benefit is normally employment income, not a business invoice. It can break the 32.2 conditions.
8. **Beckham for part of the year, exit/exclusion.** This document does not allow mixing the 210/151 and the 100 by month. First the regime for the whole period is set under the rules of art. 93. Only then is ordinary IRPF used. A change of region alone does not end the régimen Beckham and does not move it to the regional scale.
9. **Move in N, rent in two regions.** A single tax residence for N. Regional expenses are checked under the rules of the chosen region. The address on 31 December does not replace the days. You must track a return to the previous region in the following years for the purposes of 72.3.
10. **Rural Asturias.** Continuing the earlier activity after an ordinary change of address is not a new alta with the automatic 1 000 € benefit. Not all villages are in Decreto 83/2025. For the move, working remotely does not automatically prove the work reason for the move.
11. **Assets and income abroad.** Income from a Russian account/broker is taxed in ordinary IRPF, even if the account is <50 000 € and the 720 is not required. Sanctions/difficulties in transferring money are not equal to a tax exemption. Possible double taxation is checked separately under the relations in force between Spain and Russia.
12. **Loss/high withholdings.** [07] of the 130 can be negative. [12] is limited to 0, but an unused [13] can create a negative [19]. The calculation must distinguish these two negative values. An instalment payment paid in excess is refunded in the 100.
13. **Withholding not paid in by the client.** The supplier has a special right to count the legal withholding in the circumstances of art. 99.5 LIRPF. However, you cannot automatically assume that the tax was withheld without a document and without analysing the cause. Store separately the gross amount, the amount really withheld, the legal withholding and the certificate.
14. **Minimums and regional benefits.** The state minimum and the regional minimum can be different. The carry of the remainder to savings is done with two independent calculations. A regional deduction without an expressly provided refundable regime does not create a negative regional tax amount.
15. **Earlier forms and corrections.** Do not add the original 130 and the corrected one as two instalment payments. For the 2025 100, the rectifying self-assessment (autoliquidación rectificativa) applies. The HTML help about the old complementarias may not reflect the new procedure. The tax logic and the correction mechanism must be versioned separately.

## Doubtful points

- **Two errors/contradictions found in the HTML instruction of the 130.** The text for casilla 19 says «the result of subtracting the amount of casilla 17 from the amount of casilla 18», but the approved printed form expressly sets «Resultado de la autoliquidación ( 17 – 18 )» (result of the self-assessment). In the same place, for section I, after «with the maximum limit of 660,14 euros for each quarter», the text «(the maximum deduction limit for this item will be 660,14 euros per year)» has been inserted. The **RIRPF 110.3.d.1º** in force and the form («maximum: 660,14 euros per quarter») confirm the quarterly limit for direct estimation. The annual limit belongs to another branch. The engine must use the law/the form. The formulas above already take this discrepancy into account.
- **Modelo 100 for 2026: numbers and format UNVERIFIED.** As of the control date there is no confirmed final form for tax year 2026/campaign 2027 in the sources studied. The 2026 legal rates are confirmed, but the 2026 export cannot be declared ready with the 2025 mapping alone. The 130 instruction mentions previous-year casillas 2021–2026, but this does not replace the publication of the 2026 Modelo 100 itself.
- **Rounding of ties: UNVERIFIED as the exact Renta WEB implementation.** The sources require two decimal places, but they do not reveal the algorithm for all cent fractions at the boundary. Here a reasonable Decimal half-up is set expressly. Before the production export, compare the cases at the cent boundary with the official calculation. The numerical example does not depend on ties.
- **Other assets/intangibles and depreciation incentives.** The basic table and the ≤300 free depreciation are confirmed. Special ERD accelerated depreciation, new vehicles/energy, assets from earlier years and special intangibles need a separate basis. Do not replace any regime with 26%. Do not promise that a single table universally covers a specific purchase.
- **Exceptions of the 720/721.** There are exceptions for assets properly identified in established accounting and for other categories of obliged persons. Do not automatically assume that ordinary EDS books are sufficient accounting for the exemption. A specific broker/fintech/custodian, a life insurance without surrender, a trust/beneficiary, and reinvestment sequences require classification under the RGAT/the FAQ. With unknown data, the status is `requires_review`, not «no need to file».
- **Compatibility between the personal regional deduction and the business expense.** There is no established universal rule that allows using the same invoice twice for each regional benefit. For living expenses/office rent, separate the personal part and the business part and check the special regime before automatic application. Mark doubtful compatibility as **UNVERIFIED**. Do not present it as a guaranteed saving.
- **Asturias, age 30 in art. 14 sexdecies.** The consolidated literal text contains an overlap: «1.500 euros for taxpayers aged between 26 and 30 years» and «1.000 euros for taxpayers aged between 30 and 35 years». However, the AEAT Manual 2025 expressly interprets the second group as **31–35**, with the higher cap if in the year the person falls into two groups. The specification uses the AEAT clarification and keeps the reference so that the result can be explained.
- **Legislative changes after 08.10.2026.** 2026 is not yet closed: new or retroactive changes are possible. For now, the confirmed change for 2027 in this area is the Valencia scale. It does not mean that «all 2027 taxes are known». Neither the 2024 increase of the benefit for salaries, nor the old 2024 rates, nor the current 2026 Valencia scale must overwrite the 2025 calculation.
- **Limit of the example, not a hidden assumption.** Without the age, home/move expenses, other income, the start of activity and the distribution of operations by quarter, there is no single numerical answer. For the required example, all the missing parameters are set expressly. No builds/tests were run. The example was calculated arithmetically and was not checked by filing it in Renta WEB. Before implementation, the main agent must check the control example, the bracket limits and the negative-carry of the 130 with the official service.
