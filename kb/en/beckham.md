## Summary

- Beckham = a residente fiscal in Spain who opts to pay IRPF under the IRNR rules «sin establecimiento permanente» (art. 93.2 LIRPF). The annual return is **Modelo 151**, not Modelo 100. The option, the waiver and the exclusion use **Modelo 149**.
- Duration: the year in which tax residence is acquired + the 5 following years (up to 6 years in total). The "non-resident" condition covers the 5 previous tax periods (from 2023; before 2023 it was 10).
- An autónomo enters the regime ONLY as an **actividad emprendedora** (art. 93.1.b).3º: informe ENISA under art. 70 Ley 14/2013; for non-EU citizens, an autorización de residencia under art. 69 Ley 14/2013 BEFORE the move) or as a **profesional altamente cualificado** for startups / I+D+i (4º). Ordinary self-employment (including the nómada digital-autónomo and cuenta propia) = establecimiento permanente. Such a person is not admitted to the regime. A person already in the regime faces **exclusión in the same year** (DGT V2248-24, V1274-25).
- Tax on activity income: **24 % up to 600 000 €, 47 % above**. It applies to the "rendimiento neto" under art. 24.2 TRLIRNR: ingresos íntegros minus ONLY gastos de personal + aprovisionamientos + suministros. Other expenses (software, coworking, equipment, gestoría services) and **the person's own RETA cuota are NOT deductible** (DGT V1112-25). A negative result is not offset.
- **All** income from the emprendedora activity and from employment is deemed obtained in Spain (art. 93.2.b). Invoices to foreign clients are taxed at 24 %, the same as invoices to Spanish clients. Foreign withholding tax → deducción por doble imposición with a limit of 30 % of the cuota (casillas B[12]/[13], G[27]).
- Passive foreign income (dividends, interest, capital gains outside Spain) is not taxed in Spain. Spanish savings income: scale 19/21/23/27/30 % (30 % from 2025, Ley 7/2024).
- **Modelo 130 is not filed.** Modelo 151 has no casilla for pagos fraccionados. The pagos a cuenta of the regime are only retenciones/ingresos a cuenta under the IRNR rules (art. 93.2.f, RIRPF 114). UNVERIFIED as to a direct DGT consulta, but it follows from the structure of the rules and of the form.
- Retención by Spanish clients who are empresarios: **24 %** on actividades **profesionales** (IAE sección 2). There is no retención on actividades empresariales (sección 1). The invoice must state the retención rate. The payer declares it through **Modelo 216 / 296** (IRNR), not 111/190.
- IVA is not affected: 303/390/349/ROI work as for an ordinary autónomo. RETA is paid as usual (the regime does not affect SS).
- Modelo 720 is not filed (AEAT FAQ). Modelo 721 (crypto abroad): the FAQ gives no exemption → file it above 50 000 €. Patrimonio (714) and ITSGF (718) apply by **obligación real** (Spanish assets only). The taxpayer may opt to apply the rules of the CA of residence.
- The region (Valencia/Asturias) does not affect Beckham IRPF. The scale is uniform. Modelo 151 has no autonómicas deducciones. Casilla [20] = the CA with the most days in the year. The region affects only Patrimonio (the option for the CA rules) and ISD.
- A Beckham taxpayer **is not considered a resident for CDI purposes** (AEAT). A certificado de residencia «a efectos de convenio» is not issued. Only the general certificate (anexo 9) is issued (RIRPF art. 120).
- Deadline for the Modelo 149 opción: **6 months from the fecha de inicio de actividad in the alta de Seguridad Social**. Renuncia: November–December of the previous year. Exclusión: 1 month from the date of the breach. A person who waived the regime or was excluded cannot opt again.

## Rules

1. **Who may opt (contribuyente principal).** An individual who became a Spanish tax resident because of a move, when all of these conditions are met: (a) the person was not a resident in the 5 previous tax periods; (b) the move, in the year the regime starts or in the previous year, was caused by one of the reasons 1º–4º; (c) the person does not obtain income through an establecimiento permanente in Spain, except in cases 3º and 4º. In force since 01.01.2023 (Ley 28/2022 DF 3ª). Source: art. 93.1 LIRPF; AEAT Manual Renta 2025 «Ámbito de aplicación» (links above).
2. **Reasons for the move (art. 93.1.b).** 1º contrato de trabajo (including teletrabajo «por cuenta ajena»; the visado de teletrabajo internacional under Ley 14/2013 counts as evidence); 2º adquisición de la condición de administrador (any % of ownership; for an entidad patrimonial the holding must be < 25 %, art. 18 LIS); 3º actividad económica **emprendedora** (art. 70 Ley 14/2013); 4º profesional altamente cualificado for empresas emergentes (art. 3 Ley 28/2022) or for I+D+i, with remuneration > 40 % of all rendimientos empresariales/profesionales/trabajo. Source: art. 93.1.b LIRPF; RIRPF art. 113.
3. **Actividad emprendedora (RIRPF 113, AEAT Manual 2025).** An activity that is «innovadora y/o de especial interés económico para España» and is qualified by a favourable informe ENISA (art. 70 Ley 14/2013; innovation criteria: Orden PCM/825/2023 art. 4.3). Non-EU citizens need an **autorización de residencia para actividad empresarial (art. 69 Ley 14/2013) before the move**. EU/EEA citizens need an informe ENISA requested before the move through DG Industria y PYME (issued within 10 días hábiles). If the person later starts a DIFFERENT emprendedora activity, that activity needs its own informe ENISA. When filing Modelo 149, the informe ENISA is not needed if the autorización under art. 69 is attached (Instrucciones 149, apartado 4.A.3).
4. **Establecimiento permanente = exclusion.** Any actividad económica of an autónomo that does not fall under 3º/4º means income «mediante EP». This breaches art. 93.1.c → exclusión with effect in the same período impositivo; the 149 communication is due within 1 month. DGT V2248-24 (21.10.2024; an employee became an autónomo developer for foreign clients and was excluded): https://www.iberley.es/resoluciones/resolucion-vinculante-direccion-general-tributos-v2248-24-21-10-2024-11663247 ; DGT V1274-25 (10.07.2025): https://www.iberley.es/resoluciones/resolucion-vinculante-direccion-general-tributos-v1274-25-10-07-2025-11705828 . AEAT Manual 2025 (precisión): a socio who provides services to the company through an EP, beyond the functions of administrador, breaches 93.1.c.
5. **Duration of the regime and the first year.** The year in which residence is acquired (the first calendar year in which the stay after the move exceeds 183 days) + the 5 following years. RIRPF 115; Instrucciones 151 «Duración». Income from activity BEFORE the date of the move or AFTER the communication of fin de desplazamiento (149, casilla 37/38) is not deemed obtained under the regime (RIRPF 114.2.a; DGT V1112-25: a bonus for work abroad before the move is not taxed).
6. **Opción: Modelo 149.** Filed electronically (certificado/Cl@ve). The NIF and the Censo (036/037) are needed first. First complete the procedure «Aportar documentación necesaria para optar» → the registration number goes into casilla 32. Deadline: **6 months from the fecha de inicio de actividad in the alta SS** (or from the document on keeping the foreign SS / the document on the start of activity, if alta SS is not mandatory). An emprendedor attaches: alta SS (RETA), autorización de residencia, informe ENISA (if there is no autorización under art. 69). AEAT issues a documento acreditativo within 10 días hábiles. Source: RIRPF 116, 119; Instrucciones 149. Missing the 6-month deadline = loss of the right (UNVERIFIED: reports about a 2025 STS «plazo improrrogable» come only from a secondary source).
7. **Renuncia / exclusión.** Renuncia: Modelo 149 in November–December of the year before the year in which it takes effect (RIRPF 117). Exclusión: automatic in the year the conditions are breached; Modelo 149 within 1 month (RIRPF 118). In both cases the person cannot opt again.
8. **Family (contribuyentes asociados, art. 93.3).** The spouse, children < 25 years old (of any age if disabled), or the other parent of the children without marriage. They move with the principal or later, up to the end of the principal's first year in the regime. They become residents. They meet (a) and (c). **The sum of their bases liquidables < the base liquidable of the principal** in every year. Their Modelo 149 deadline is 6 months from entry or the principal's deadline, whichever is later. Their regime lasts as long as the principal has it.
9. **Calculation of the tax (art. 93.2; RIRPF 114).** Under TRLIRNR for income sin EP, with these special features: arts. 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 14 TRLIRNR do not apply (but rendimientos del trabajo en especie are exempt under art. 14.1.a); all income from trabajo and from the emprendedora activity is deemed Spanish; income is taxed cumulatively for the calendar year **without offsetting** between items; there are two bases: «ahorro» (art. 25.1.f TRLIRNR: dividends, interest, gains from transfers) and «general» (everything else).
10. **Base for the actividad económica (epígrafe B Modelo 151, art. 24.2 TRLIRNR, art. 5 RIRNR).** Rendimiento neto = Ingresos íntegros − gastos de personal (salaries and SS of staff hired in Spain, with proof that their retenciones were paid) − aprovisionamiento de materiales (incorporated into works in Spain) − suministros (non-storable supplies consumed in Spain). Only invoices that comply with the Reglamento de facturación count. **Other expenses are not deductible.** A negative result → casilla [07]; it is not offset and not carried forward. Source: Instrucciones 151, Página 5.
11. **The person's own RETA is not deductible.** DGT V1112-25 (26.06.2025): the deduction of expenses under art. 24.6 TRLIRNR is available only to residents of another EU/EEA state. A Beckham taxpayer is a resident of Spain → SS contributions are not deductible. Overview: https://ga-p.com/publicaciones/regimen-fiscal-de-impatriados-tributacion-de-un-bonus-percibido-en-el-primer-ano-de-aplicacion-del-regimen-beckham-y-deducibilidad-de-los-gastos-de-seguridad-social/ (the consulta is about an employee's SS; for RETA it applies by analogy; UNVERIFIED as to a direct consulta on RETA; in any case art. 24.2 has no category «cotizaciones propias»).
12. **Scales (art. 93.2.e).** General: 24 % up to 600 000 €, 47 % above. Ahorro (2025 onwards): 19/21/23/27/30 % (from 01.01.2025, Ley 7/2024 DF 7.3). As of 08.10.2026 the BOE has no changes for 2026.
13. **Cuota diferencial (art. 93.2.f; RIRPF 114).** Cuota íntegra − deducción por donativos (IRPF rules) − deducción por doble imposición internacional (art. 80 LIRPF) for trabajo and emprendedora income obtained and taxed abroad, limited to 30 % of the part of the cuota íntegra for such income − retenciones/ingresos a cuenta − IRNR cuotas paid for the same year (casilla 34). There is no mínimo personal/familiar, no reducciones, no deducción vivienda, no autonómicas, no declaración conjunta.
14. **Retenciones (RIRPF 114.3).** Under the IRNR rules sin EP. Rendimientos del trabajo: 24 % (47 % above 600 000 € from one payer). Rendimientos de actividades económicas: retención ONLY on actividades **profesionales**; the general IRNR rate is 24 % (art. 25.1.a TRLIRNR; AEAT novedades RD 1008/2023). The invoice states the retención rate. The payer (a Spanish empresario/profesional/entidad) files 216 (quarterly) and 296 (annual). Foreign clients do not withhold.
15. **Pagos fraccionados.** The 130/131 rules (art. 99.7 LIRPF, RIRPF 109–112) do not apply in the regime. The debt is calculated under TRLIRNR sin EP, which has no pagos fraccionados. RIRPF 114 mentions only retenciones/ingresos a cuenta. In Modelo 151, casilla [33] adds up only the retenciones of epígrafes A1/A2/B/C/D/E1; there is no place for a pago fraccionado. → **Do not file Modelo 130.** UNVERIFIED: no direct DGT/INFORMA answer was found. The practice of an online service for autónomos (FAQ) confirms «otros modelos trimestrales» without mentioning 130.
16. **Obligaciones formales of the autónomo.** RIRPF 114 → art. 6 RIRNR: keep libros registro de ingresos y gastos; keep the invoices. Censo 036/037, IAE, ROI (if there are EU clients): as for an ordinary autónomo.
17. **IVA.** The regime concerns only IRPF. The autónomo is a sujeto pasivo of IVA under the general rules (303/390/349, inversión del sujeto pasivo for EU B2B, no sujeto for non-EU B2B under the reglas de localización). See the IVA knowledge base page.
18. **Modelo 720 / 721.** 720: not obliged (AEAT FAQ 720: «no resultan obligados», because they are not taxed on worldwide income; a spouse/children outside the regime are obliged): https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-720-decla_____sobre-bienes-derechos-extranjero_/preguntas-frecuentes/contribuyentes-obligados-declarar.html . 721: the FAQ defines who is obliged under art. 42 quater RGAT, with no exception for art. 93: https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-721-decla-sobre-monedas-extranjero/preguntas-frecuentes-sobre-modelo-721.html → the exemption is UNVERIFIED; cautious: file it if the balance at a foreign custodian on 31.12 exceeds 50 000 €.
19. **Impuesto sobre el Patrimonio.** Art. 93.1.c: sujeción by **obligación real**, that is, only assets located or exercisable in Spain. The taxpayer has the right (an option) to apply the rules of the CA of habitual residence under the IRPF rules (Ley 22/2009), casilla [12] Modelo 714. The return is mandatory if there is a cuota a ingresar or the assets exceed 2 000 000 €. Source: AEAT Manual Patrimonio «Sujetos pasivos por obligación real»: https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/patrimonio-2023/c-2-cuestiones-generales/quienes-estan-sujetos-i-patrimonio/sujetos-pasivos-obligacion-real.html ; the 714 service for art. 93: https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/no-residentes/impuesto-sobre-patrimonio/contribuyentes-reg-especial-art-93-patrimonio/modelo-714-impuesto-sobre-patrimonio_.html
20. **ITSGF (Modelo 718).** In force for 2025 and 2026 (extended by RDL 8/2023 «hasta que se revise la tributación patrimonial»): https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2023-26452 . Obligación real: Spanish assets only; mínimo exento 700 000 €; threshold 3 000 000 € of patrimonio neto; filed only when there is a cuota a ingresar; Orden HAC/652/2026 updated Modelo 718: https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2026-14011 . Not relevant for a profile with an income of ~60 k€.
21. **Certificado de residencia fiscal.** AEAT: Beckham taxpayers «no tienen la consideración de residentes a efectos de la aplicación de un CDI». Only the general certificado de residencia fiscal en España is issued (RIRPF 120.1, anexo 9 Orden HAC/3626/2003), not the one «a efectos de convenio». Consequence: a foreign client or bank may apply its own withholding to payments without CDI benefits → see the DII deducción (rule 13).
22. **Owned vivienda habitual.** A Beckham taxpayer pays imputación de rentas inmobiliarias (1,1 %/2 % of the valor catastral) even on the home they live in (TEAC 17.07.2025, RG 00/03697/2025, unificación de criterio; reflected in the Manual Renta 2025). Epígrafe A1, clave 09, base general 24 %.
23. **Administrador SL (route 2º).** The move is caused by the appointment as administrador. The % of ownership does not matter, except for an entidad patrimonial (< 25 %). Retribución de administrador = rendimientos del trabajo (clave 03), retención 24 % by the company through 216/296. If the administrador personally performs the whole activity as a «one-man company» without a structure → risk of EP → exclusion (DGT V1200-26, overview https://ponter.es/regimen-impatriados-espana-requisitos-ventajas/ ; the text of the consulta is UNVERIFIED). Dividends from a Spanish SL: base ahorro (A2, clave 04), retención 19 %.
24. **First year and IRNR cuotas.** If IRNR cuotas (Modelo 210) were paid in the same year, before residence was acquired, on income included in Modelo 151, they are deducted in casilla [34], broken down in [35]–[40].

## Formulas for the engine

All amounts are in euros with two decimals. The casillas of Modelo 151 are filled in with the parte entera and 2 decimales. Rounding of intermediate values: store in cents, round half-up to 0,01 when writing to a casilla. Tipo medio: 2 decimales (%).

```
INPUT (year Y, taxpayer in the art. 93 regime for the whole year or part of it):
  regime_start_date, regime_end_date   # from 149 / the year of residence
  activity_type ∈ {emprendedora(1), startup_hq(2), idi(3)}; iae_epigrafe; is_profesional (IAE sección 2 → True)
  invoices[]:  {date, client_country, base_sin_iva, retencion_practicada (0, or 24% if the Spanish client is obligado a retener and is_profesional)}
  expenses[]:  {date, category ∈ {personal, aprovisionamiento, suministro, otro}, amount, factura_ok}
  reta_paid    # informative; NOT deductible
  foreign_tax_on_activity[]: {gross_income_taxed_abroad, tax_paid_abroad}
  spanish_savings_income[]: dividends/interest {gross, retencion}
  spanish_capital_gains[] ...
  imputed_property[]: {valor_catastral, revisado_10y, days_owned, pct}
  donations[]
  irnr_cuotas_paid_same_year (Modelo 210 before residence)

STEP 0. Filter by regime:
  only invoices/income with date within [regime_start_date, regime_end_date]
  (activity income before the move / after fin de desplazamiento is outside 151; RIRPF 114.2.a)

STEP 1. Epígrafe B (one apartado per activity):
  B.01 tipo_renta = 12 if is_profesional else 10
  B.10 codigo_actividad = activity_type (1 = emprendedora)
  B.11 IAE
  B.02 ingresos_integros = Σ invoices.base_sin_iva         # ALL clients, including foreign ones (art. 93.2.b)
  B.03 gastos_personal   = Σ expenses[category=personal, factura_ok]
  B.04 aprov_suministros = Σ expenses[category ∈ {aprovisionamiento, suministro}, factura_ok]
       # «otro» (software, coworking, equipment, gestoría, RETA) → 0; do NOT deduct
  neto = B.02 − B.03 − B.04
  if neto ≥ 0: B.05 = neto; B.07 = 0  else: B.05 = 0; B.07 = −neto   # losses are not offset
  B.06 retenciones = Σ invoices.retencion_practicada
  B.12 = Σ foreign_tax_on_activity.gross_income_taxed_abroad ; B.13 = Σ tax_paid_abroad  (only if activity_type = 1)
  B.08 = Σ B.05 over all apartados; B.09 = Σ B.06

STEP 2. Epígrafe A1 (general, except the activity): rendimientos del trabajo (clave 01/03), imputación inmobiliaria (clave 09):
  A1.06 (inmueble) = valor_catastral × (1.1% if revisado_10y else 2%) × days_owned/365(366) × pct
  A1.15 = Σ A1.06; A1.16 = Σ A1.07 (retenciones)

STEP 3. Epígrafe A2 (ahorro): dividends/interest from a Spanish source: A2.08 = Σ gross; A2.09 = Σ retenciones.
  Epígrafes C/D/E2: ganancias (without offsetting pérdidas). E1: other ganancias general.
  Foreign dividends/interest/ganancias: do NOT include (not a Spanish source; art. 93.2.c).

STEP 4. Bases:
  F.17 base_general = A1.15 + B.08 + E1.05
  F.18 base_ahorro  = A2.08 + C.04 + D.12 + E2.06

STEP 5. Cuotas:
  G.19 = min(F.17, 600000) × 0.24 + max(F.17 − 600000, 0) × 0.47
  G.20 = escala_ahorro(F.18)   # table above: 19/21/23/27/30
  G.21 = G.19 + G.20 ; G.22 = G.23 = G.21 × 0.5   # estatal/autonómica: a purely accounting split
  G.24 = deducción donativos (Ley 49/2002 rules); G.25 = G.26 = G.24/2
  # Deducción doble imposición (only trabajo/emprendedora taxed abroad)
  cuota_liquida_previa = G.21 − G.24
  TMG = round( (cuota_liquida_previa × (G.19 / G.21)) / F.17 × 100 , 2 )   # % with 2 decimales; if G.21 = 0 → 0
  dii_candidate = min( A1.09 + B.13 , (A1.08 + B.12) × TMG/100 )
  dii_limit = 0.30 × G.19 × ((trabajo + emprendedora part of F.17) / F.17)
  G.27 = min(dii_candidate, dii_limit); G.28 = G.29 = G.27/2
  G.30 = max(G.21 − G.24 − G.27, 0) ; G.31 = G.32 = G.30/2
  G.33 = A1.16 + A2.09 + B.09 + C.05 + D.13 + E1.06      # retenciones; NO pagos fraccionados
  G.34 = irnr_cuotas_paid_same_year (split into [35]..[40] by epígrafe)
  G.43 = G.30 − G.33 − G.34 (− G.41 + G.42 only for a complementaria)
  resultado: G.43 > 0 → a ingresar; < 0 → a devolver (with the sign «−»); = 0 → cuota cero.

STEP 6. Quarterly IRPF obligations: NONE (no 130/131). The only quarterly returns are IVA (303) and, if there are employees, 111.
  If the Spanish client is obligado a retener and is_profesional: the invoice has the line «Retención IRPF/IRNR 24 % (art. 93 LIRPF)»; amount payable = base + IVA − 0.24 × base.

STEP 7. Patrimonio (714), only if there are assets in Spain: cuota > 0 or assets > 2 000 000 € → obligación real; option for the CA rules (casilla 12) of the CA with the most days in the year.

STEP 8. Casilla [20] (CA residencia) = the CA with the most days in year Y (Valencia 17, Asturias 03).

VALIDATIONS:
  - activity_type ∉ {1,2,3} → warning «activity outside art. 93.1.b.3º/4º → exclusión, 149 within 1 month, calculate year Y under the general IRPF».
  - Date of 149 > alta_SS + 6 months → regime not available.
  - Year Y > regime_start_year + 5 → switch to Modelo 100.
  - Sum of the bases of the asociados ≥ base of the principal → the asociados are excluded.
```

### Numerical example (year 2025, autónomo-emprendedor, developer profile, IAE sección 2 → profesional)

Input: invoices of 60 000 € (of which 10 000 € to a Spanish client-empresa with a 24 % retención, and 50 000 € to foreign clients without retención). Expenses of 6 000 € (software, coworking, laptop, gestoría: category «otro», not deductible). Assume that 600 € of them are suministros (internet/electricity) with facturas. RETA 3 600 €.

```
B.02 = 60 000,00
B.03 = 0,00
B.04 = 600,00                 # suministros only; strict variant 0,00
B.05 = 59 400,00              # strict variant 60 000,00
B.06 = 2 400,00               # 24 % × 10 000
F.17 = 59 400,00 ; F.18 = 0
G.19 = 59 400 × 0,24 = 14 256,00   (strict: 14 400,00)
G.21 = G.30 = 14 256,00
G.33 = 2 400,00
G.43 = 14 256,00 − 2 400,00 = 11 856,00 a ingresar (strict: 12 000,00)
RETA 3 600 € is paid, but it does not reduce the tax. Effective IRPF burden: 23,8 % of revenue.
```
For comparison (a reference point; the calculation is in the IRPF autónomo knowledge base page): under estimación directa simplificada the base would be ≈ 60 000 − 6 000 − 3 600 − 7 % gastos difícil justificación (≤ 2 000) ≈ 48 400 €. It would be taxed on the progressive scale with the mínimo personal. At ~50 k€ the result is comparable to Beckham or lower. The engine must show both figures when the regime is chosen.

## Edge cases

1. **Incomplete first year.** Move in September 2024 (< 183 days in 2024) → residence from 2025 → regime 2025–2030. Income for September–December 2024 is IRNR (210) under the non-resident rules. Move in February 2025 → regime 2025–2030, but activity income before the date of the move is not included in Modelo 151 (RIRPF 114.2.a).
2. **Move between comunidades autónomas in the middle of the year.** It does not affect the tax. Casilla [20] = the CA with the most days. Patrimonio: the option for the CA rules applies to the same CA. Cambio de domicilio: Modelo 030 (or casilla 13 in Modelo 151).
3. **Change or addition of an activity.** A new activity without an informe ENISA (for example, side consulting work) = EP → exclusión for the whole year + switch to Modelo 100 with the progressive scale. A new informe ENISA is needed before the start (AEAT Manual 2025).
4. **Retención by a Spanish client.** Only if the payer is an empresario/profesional/entidad (obligado a retener) and the activity is profesional (sección 2 IAE). A private individual as a client: no retención. A client may, by mistake, withhold 15 %/7 % as under IRPF and file Modelo 111. For the engine: take into account the amount actually withheld (casilla [07] «efectivamente practicadas»), but warn the user that the correct rate is 24 % and the correct form is 216.
5. **A foreign client withheld tax at source** (for example, 10–20 % on services in some countries): doble imposición, casillas B.12/B.13 → G.27, limit 30 %. The CDI cannot be applied (no resident status under the convention).
6. **Loss-making year.** B.07 > 0 → tax 0 on the activity, but the loss is not carried forward. Other income (for example, imputación inmobiliaria) is still taxed.
7. **Buying a home.** Imputación 1,1 %/2 % × 24 % every year (TEAC 2025). On a sale, the buyer withholds 3 % (211); the ganancia goes to epígrafe D under the ahorro scale; the exención por reinversión is available (clave 21).
8. **Spouse-asociado with their own income.** If their base liquidable ≥ the base of the principal (for example, the principal has a loss-making year) → the asociado is excluded for that year.
9. **Foreign dividends, interest, crypto gains.** They are not declared in Modelo 151 (not a Spanish source), but Modelo 721 may be required. Patrimonio: not included (obligación real).
10. **Remote employee + autónomo.** Beckham through route 1º + any autónomo activity (not 3º/4º) → exclusion. The engine must block this combination.
11. **SL + administrador.** If the administrador personally provides services to the SL's clients (one-man company) → risk of EP (V1200-26). Safer: only the administrador's retribución (clave 03, 24 %) + dividends (ahorro). A salary under an employment contract with one's own SL is also rendimientos del trabajo. A socio-profesional who invoices their own SL breaches 93.1.c (AEAT Manual 2025).
12. **Missed 149 deadline.** The regime is not available. The whole year falls under Modelo 100/130. Engine: the date of the RETA alta + 6 months = a hard deadline; send reminders well in advance.
13. **End of the 6th year.** From the following year: Modelo 100, 130, 720 (above 50 k€ abroad), Patrimonio by obligación personal (worldwide assets).
14. **RETA regularisation for Beckham.** TGSS (consulta Valencia 02/2026) reports that there is no IRPF data exchange for Modelo 151 filers. The regularisation uses a placeholder (a base of 1 000 €; from 2026, 1 424,40 €). The question is under national consultation: https://www.cograsova.es/images/adjuntosmail/2026/20260226/consultas.pdf — UNVERIFIED (data from KbReta). Engine: show RETA based on real income (B.05 as the «rendimiento neto»? It does not match the IRPF net: there is no deduction of RETA and of the 7 %); mark this as an uncertainty.

## Open points

1. **Modelo 130 in the regime.** The absence of the obligation is derived from the structure of the rules and of the 151 form. No direct DGT/INFORMA answer was found. Cautious implementation: do not file Modelo 130, but calculate the expected tax and suggest setting aside 24 % every quarter (without filing). If AEAT sends a requerimiento, reply with a reference to art. 93.2.f and RIRPF 114.
2. **Deduction of RETA.** V1112-25 concerns an employee's SS. No direct consulta on the cuota de autónomo was found. The logic of art. 24.2 excludes any other expenses → do not deduct.
3. **Which expenses exactly are «suministros».** Art. 5 RIRNR: «abastecimientos que no tengan la cualidad de almacenables», consumed in Spain. Electricity/water/gas: yes. Internet/telephony: probably yes (UNVERIFIED). SaaS subscriptions, hosting: disputed; cautious: no. Engine: only the «suministro» category with the user's confirmation; by default other expenses are not deducted.
4. **Nómada digital-autónomo and ENISA.** Can a non-EU citizen with a DN visa + an informe ENISA enter through route 3º without an autorización under art. 69? RIRPF 113 requires art. 69 «con carácter previo». Cautious: not eligible; recommend switching to an autorización de emprendedor before the move.
5. **Modelo 721.** The exemption for art. 93 is confirmed neither by the FAQ nor by the DGT (there is an old V0092-14 on Modelo 720). Cautious: file it.
6. **149 deadline «improrrogable» (STS 2025).** Only secondary sources (beckhamlaw.eu, a marketing site with doubtful details, e.g. «subcode RT.40-B», «autonómicas deducciones in 151»; the official 151 instructions contain none of this). Do not rely on this site.
7. **Modelo 151 for 2026 / Orden for the 2027 campaign.** The deadlines for ejercicio 2026 will be in the Orden Renta 2026 (published ~March 2027). For now, assume «up to 30.06.2027».
8. **Retención rate for profesionales: 24 % vs 19 %.** Art. 25.1.a TRLIRNR gives 19 % to EU/EEA residents. A Beckham taxpayer is a resident of Spain, not an EU resident of another state → 24 % (confirmed by AEAT novedades RD 1008/2023). If the payer withholds 19 %, show the actual amount in Modelo 151.
9. **Tipo medio for the DII when there is an ahorro base.** The formula comes from the examples in the 151 instructions (cuota líquida previa × share G.19/G.21 ÷ F.17). When G.21 = 0 the division is undefined → DII = 0.
10. **Regional benefits.** Modelo 151 has no autonómicas deducciones (only donativos and DII). Reports about «deducciones autonómicas for impatriados (Madrid)» are UNVERIFIED and probably do not apply to Modelo 151. For Valencia/Asturias: ignore.
11. **Ley 14/2013 art. 69: authorisation periods (3 years + 2).** Outside my scope; see the visados knowledge base page (KbVisados).
12. **Verifactu.** A report about the postponement to October 2028 (ponter.es 06.10.2026) is outside the scope; check the calendar knowledge base page / BOE.
