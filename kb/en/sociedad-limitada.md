# Sociedad Limitada (SL/SLU) with one socio-administrador: taxes, reporting, bookkeeping

Valid as of: 2026-10-08. Scope: a service SL/SLU with one socio-administrador who is a Spanish tax resident; domestic and cross-border B2B operations; INCN clearly < 1 000 000 €. The examples are illustrative.

## Summary

- The SL itself pays IS (Impuesto sobre Sociedades), not the socio. The general rate is 25 %. For a "micro-enterprise" (INCN of the previous period < 1 M€) a transitional scale applies: **2025: 21 % up to 50 000 € of base / 22 % on the rest; 2026: 19 % / 21 %; 2027 and later: 17 % / 20 %** (art. 29.1 + DT 44ª LIS, Ley 7/2024). For an ERD with INCN 1–10 M€: 24 % (2025), 23 % (2026), 22 % (2027), 21 % (2028), 20 % (2029+).
- Entidad de nueva creación (ENC): 15 % in the first period with a positive base and in the next period. **It does not apply if, in the previous year, the same activity was carried out as an individual by the person who owns > 50 % of the new SL.** This means that an autónomo who "moves" his business into his own SL does not get the 15 %.
- Empresa emergente (Ley 28/2022): 15 % for four periods, deferral of IS without interest, no pagos fraccionados. This applies only with ENISA certification. For a single-person service SL it is practically unreachable.
- Three modelo 202 returns (1–20 April, October, December). By default each one = 18 % of the cuota of the last filed modelo 200 (minus retenciones). If this cuota is ≤ 0 (including the first year), modelo 202 is not filed at all. The art. 40.3 option (based on the current base) is chosen through modelo 036 in February.
- Modelo 200 is always filed, even with a loss or zero activity: 1–25 July (for 2025: until **27.07.2026**, domiciliación until 22.07.2026).
- Pay of the administrador "for the position": fixed retención **35 %**, or **19 %** if the INCN of the last closed period is < 100 000 €; modelo 111 + 190 (clave E). If the socio personally provides professional services to the SL (sección 2ª IAE, e.g. 763 programmers) and is in RETA, under art. 27.1 LIRPF this is an **actividad económica**. He issues an invoice to the SL with retención 15 % (7 % in the first 3 years), not a nómina.
- Dividends: retención 19 %, modelo 123 (quarterly) + 193 (January). For the recipient: base del ahorro 19/21/23/27/30 %. Under Beckham, dividends from a Spanish SL are Spanish-source income. They are taxed on the same ahorro scale in modelo 151.
- Beckham + SL: entry through the position of administrador is possible with any shareholding (the < 25 % limit applies only to an entidad patrimonial). Income from one's own SL as a professional (art. 27.1) = actividad económica. It is allowed only on entry under the "emprendedor" ground (93.1.b.3º). The region does not matter for IS or for Beckham.
- Registro Mercantil: legalización de libros ≤ 4 months after the year closes (30 April), formulación of cuentas ≤ 3 months (31 March), aprobación ≤ 6 months (30 June), depósito ≤ 1 month after approval (≈ 30 July) + hoja de titularidad real. ICAC fine for not filing the deposit: 1 200–60 000 €. Cierre registral after one year.
- RETA autónomo societario: min. base 1 000 € (2025) → **1 424,40 € (2026, final after regularisation)**. Contribution ≈ 314 €/month (2025) → ≈ 449 €/month (2026). If the SL pays it, this is retribución en especie of the administrador (DGT V0067-19, V2121-25).
- Verifactu for IS taxpayers legally starts on 01.01.2027 (RDL 15/2025). On 05.10.2026 Hacienda announced a postponement to October 2028. This is not yet in the BOE.
- Economics: with full payout of profit, an SL beats an autónomo (ordinary IRPF) from roughly 90–100 k€ of revenue. At 60 k€ the result is parity or a loss. Under Beckham (24 % flat) an autónomo is better than an SL in all scenarios, except when profit is reinvested inside the SL.

## Rules

1. **Taxpayer and period.** The SL is an IS contribuyente from the moment it acquires legal personality. The period = ejercicio social (usually a year) and ends on 31.12. The first period runs from the date of the escritura to 31.12 (a sociedad en formación that is later registered is subject to IS from the date of the escritura). In force permanently. Ley 27/2014 (LIS) art. 7, 27: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-12328
2. **IS rates** (periods starting in 2025/2026/2027): see the table in "Figures". Micro-enterprise = INCN of the *previous* period < 1 M€ and not an entidad patrimonial. The 50 000 € threshold is reduced proportionally if the period is < 12 months. LIS art. 29.1, DT 44ª (wording of Ley 7/2024 DF 8ª): https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2024-26694 ; AEAT table: https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/impuesto-sobre-sociedades/que-base-imponible-se-determina-sociedades/tipo-impositivo.html
3. **ENC 15 %** (art. 29.1 párr. 2): the first period with BI > 0 and the next one. Exclusions: entidad patrimonial; a group under art. 42 CCom; the activity was previously carried out by a vinculada person; or, in the year before incorporation, the activity was carried out by an individual who then owns > 50 %. If the law provides a lower rate, the lower rate applies. TEAC criterion: the conditions are checked on the devengo of each of the two periods.
4. **Empresa emergente** (Ley 28/2022 art. 7–8): 15 % in the first period with BI > 0 and the three following periods; aplazamiento of 12 and 6 months without guarantees or interest; exemption from pagos fraccionados for the 2 following periods. This applies only with the status certified by ENISA and entered in the RM. https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-21739 ; AEAT: https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/impuesto-sobre-sociedades/novedades-impuesto-sobre-sociedades/novedades-normativa________.html
5. **Reserva de capitalización** (art. 25 LIS, from 01.01.2025): the BI is reduced by 20 % of the increase in fondos propios (23 / 26,5 / 30 % if the average headcount grows by ≥2 %, ≥5 %, >10 % and is kept for 3 years). Limit: 20 % of the positive BI previa (25 % if INCN < 1 M€). The remainder is carried forward to the 2 following periods. The FP increase and the indisponible reserve must be kept for 3 years. The FP increase excludes the result of the year, contributions of socios, reserva legal/estatutaria, reserva de nivelación. https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/manual-sociedades-2025/principales-novedades-impuesto-sobre-sociedades-2025/reserva-capitalizacion.html
6. **Reserva de nivelación** (art. 105 LIS): only ERD (including micro on the art. 29.1 párr. 1 scale; **not** ENC at 15 %). Minus up to 10 % of the positive BI (≤ 1 M€). A mandatory indisponible reserve is required. The amount is added back against BIN of the next 5 years; any remainder is added back in the 5th year. It is also taken into account in pagos fraccionados 40.3. https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/manual-sociedades-2025/capitulo-10-regimenes-tributarios-especiales-ii/regimen-especial-empresas-reducida-dimension/incentivos-fiscales/reserva-nivelacion.html
7. **BIN** (art. 26): offset up to 70 % of BI previa, but not less than 1 M€ per year. For our SL this means practically no limit. Carry-forward has no time limit.
8. **Non-deductible expenses** (art. 15 LIS): retribución de fondos propios (dividends), IS itself, fines/sanctions/recargos, donativos y liberalidades (except atenciones a clientes ≤ 1 % INCN and promotion), expenses contrarios al ordenamiento, operations with "paraísos". Pay of the administrador for alta dirección functions or under an employment contract is not a liberalidad under the direct text of 15.e). Supreme Court (STS 13.03.2024 rec. 9078/2022; 30.04.2024 rec. 7481/2022; 13.06.2024 rec. 78/2023): the lack of a clause in the statutes does not by itself make real, recorded pay non-deductible. Cautious practice: statutes with "cargo retribuido" + a decision of the socio único on the amount (art. 217 LSC). https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-12328 ; Supreme Court review: https://www.poderjudicial.es/stfls/TRIBUNAL%20SUPREMO/ACUERDOS%20y%20ESTUDIOS%20DOCTRINALES/FICHERO/20240722%20Cr%C3%B3nicaJurisprudencia%202023-2024%20Sala%203.pdf
9. **Amortización** (art. 12, 102, 103 LIS): according to the art. 12.1 table (equipos para procesos de información 25 %/8 years, software 33 %/6, mobiliario 10 %/20, equipos electrónicos 20 %/10). ERD: accelerated, coefficient ×2. Bienes de escaso valor ≤ 300 €/unit: free, up to 25 000 €/year. Libertad de amortización when headcount grows (120 000 € × Δ plantilla).
10. **Operaciones vinculadas** (art. 18 LIS): a socio with ≥ 25 % and the administrador are vinculados (except for pay for the position itself). Valuation at market value. Safe harbour for a socio-profesional (18.6): ≥ 75 % of the SL income is professional; ≥ 75 % of the result before deducting these payments goes to the socios; each socio gets ≥ 1,5× the average salary of comparable employees or ≥ 5× IPREM. **Modelo 232** (1–30 November) is mandatory if operations of one type and method with vinculados are > 50 % of INCN. For a single-person SL that pays the socio as a professional, this is almost always the case. https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/manual-sociedades-2025/capitulo-08-operaciones-personas-entidades-vinculadas/obligaciones-informacion/informacion-sobre-operaciones-vinculadas-modelo-232/obligados-presentar-modelo-232.html
11. **Pagos fraccionados** (art. 40 LIS): modalidad 40.2: 18 % of (cuota íntegra − deducciones − bonificaciones − retenciones − ingresos a cuenta) of the last modelo 200 whose filing deadline expired by 1 April/October/December. If the result is ≤ 0, no filing is needed (except INCN > 6 M€). Modalidad 40.3: an option through modelo 036 in February (mandatory with INCN > 6 M€). The base covers 3/9/11 months; the percentage = floor(5/7 × rate). https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/impuesto-sobre-sociedades/modelo-202-is-i_____resencia-territorio-fraccionado_/instrucciones/Instrucciones-para-2025.html
12. **Modelo 200** (art. 124): 25 calendar days after 6 months from the end of the period. When the year = the calendar year: 1–25 July (moved to the next working day). It is always filed, including with a loss, zero, or inactivity. https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/folleto-actividades-economicas/4-impuesto-sobre-sociedades/4_9-declaracion/4_9_1-plazo-presentacion.html
13. **Retención of the administrador** (art. 101.2 LIRPF, art. 80.1.3º RIRPF): 35 %; 19 % if the INCN of the last period closed before the payment is < 100 000 € (exactly 100 000 already means 35 %). Modelo 111 quarterly, modelo 190 annual, clave E (E.01/E.02: asimilado cuenta ajena; E.03: in RETA and INCN < 100k; E.04: all others). https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/static_files/Sede/Programas_ayuda/Retenciones/2026/CUADRO_TIPOS_RETENCION_IRPF_2026.pdf
14. **Socio-profesional** (art. 27.1 párr. 3 LIRPF): the SL provides professional services of sección 2ª IAE; the socio performs these same services for it; and the socio is in RETA (or must be in RETA: STS 1250/2025). In that case his income from the SL = rendimientos de actividades económicas. He issues an invoice to the SL with retención 15 % (7 % in the starting year and the two following years, on written notice); 111/190 clave G. IVA 21 % by default (see "Open questions"). https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c06-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-cuestiones-generales/concepto-rendimientos-actividades-economicas.html ; DGT V1500-15 (programmer, epígrafe 763): https://www.iberley.es/resoluciones/resolucion-vinculante-dgt-v1500-15-18-05-2015-1416571
15. **RETA paid by the SL** is retribución en especie of the administrador/socio (dineraria if the money was given to him). It is part of the retención base, it is reported in modelo 190, and the recipient deducts it as SS. DGT V0067-19, V2121-25: https://bm.consulting/es/consultas-vinculantes/V2121-25/
16. **Dividends** (art. 101.4 LIRPF, 25.1.a): retención 19 %, modelo 123 (1–20 April/July/October/January), modelo 193 (1–31 January; for 2025: until 02.02.2026). https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/folleto-actividades-economicas/7-otras-obligaciones-fiscales-retenciones/7_4-retenciones-capital-mobiliario-modelos-193.html
17. **Office rent** from an individual or a company: retención 19 %, modelo 115 (quarterly) + 180 (January). It is not needed if the rent is ≤ 900 €/year to one landlord, or the landlord is in IAE epígrafe 861 with cuota > 0 (large landlords), or it is leasing. Art. 75.3.g RIRPF.
18. **Beckham and the administrador** (art. 93.1.b.2º LIRPF, wording of Ley 28/2022): appointment as administrador is a ground for relocation with any shareholding, except for an entidad patrimonial (then the share must be < 25 %). The ground 93.1.b.3º (actividad emprendedora) is the only one that allows income through an EP/actividad económica. For a visa de emprendedor (Ley 14/2013 art. 70), the "informe ENISA" requirement is treated as met (art. 113 RIRPF). Dividends and salary from a Spanish SL are Spanish-source income → taxed in modelo 151. https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c02-irpf-cuestiones-generales/sujecion-irpf-aspectos-personales/regimen-fiscal-especial-aplicable-trabajadores-desplazados/regimen-aplicable-trabajadores-desplazados/ambito-aplicacion.html
19. **Registro Mercantil: libros** (art. 18 Ley 14/2013, Instrucción DGRN 12.02.2015): all mandatory books (Diario, Inventarios y Cuentas Anuales, Actas, Registro de socios, Registro de contratos socio único–sociedad) are legalised online in electronic format no later than 4 months after the year closes. https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2015-1481
20. **Cuentas anuales** (LSC art. 253, 164, 279, 282, 283): formulación ≤ 3 months, aprobación by the junta ≤ 6 months, depósito ≤ 1 month after approval. PYME/abreviado models apply when 2 of 3 thresholds are met (activo ≤ 4 M€, INCN ≤ 8 M€, ≤ 50 employees). The hoja de titularidad real is filed together with the depósito (RD 609/2023). Changes of the beneficial owner go to the RM within 10 days. Sanction 1 200–60 000 €. Cierre registral under art. 378 RRM after one year without a deposit. https://boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2010-10544 ; https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2023/07/11/609
21. **SLU** (LSC art. 12–17): the unipersonalidad is registered in the RM and stated on all documents ("SLU"). Decisions of the socio único go into an acta. Contracts socio único ↔ SL are made in writing and entered in the libro-registro de contratos, with a mention in the memoria. Otherwise they cannot be relied on in insolvency, and the socio is liable for the benefits obtained from them for 2 years.
22. **Capital** (Ley 18/2022 "Crea y Crece"): minimum 1 €. With capital < 3 000 €: 20 % of profit goes to the reserva legal until capital + RL ≥ 3 000 €, and the socios are jointly liable up to 3 000 € on liquidation. Otherwise the reserva legal is 10 % of profit up to 20 % of capital (art. 274 LSC).
23. **036**: alta/NIF provisional (casillas 110/111) before operations start; NIF definitivo (casilla 120) after registration in the RM. IAE epígrafe (casilla 402): an SL is in sección 1ª (e.g. 845 "Explotación electrónica por cuenta de terceros" or 843.9). Exemption from IAE for the first 2 periods and while INCN < 1 M€. Activate the retenedor obligations (111/115/123) only when they actually arise. ROI (349) for services to EU counterparties. The 40.3 option: casilla 621 (alta) in February. https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/Ayuda/guia-practica-declaracion-censal.html
24. **IVA/347/349**: the same rules as for an autónomo (see the IVA knowledge base page): 303 quarterly, 390 in January, 349 for EU operations, 347 in February (counterparties > 3 005,06 € not included in 349/190/180). Difference: Spanish clients do **not** withhold IRPF on SL invoices for services (retención on professional services applies only to individuals).
25. **Verifactu / factura electrónica**: RD 1007/2023 for IS taxpayers: adaptation by 01.01.2027 (RDL 15/2025). A Hacienda note of 05.10.2026 announces a postponement to October 2028 (not published in the BOE). B2B e-factura (RD 238/2026, Orden HAC/1028/2026): INCN > 8 M€ from 06.10.2027, everyone else from 06.10.2028, only for operations between Spanish empresarios. https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/sistemas-informaticos-facturacion-verifactu/nota-informativa-ampliacion-plazo-adaptacion-facturacion.html ; https://boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20587

## Formulas for the engine

All money is in cents or Decimal. Final amounts in casillas are euros with 2 decimals, rounding half-up (instructions for 202: "mitad de céntimo → cifra superior").

### 1. Period parameters

```
period = {start, end}                      # first period: start = fecha escritura
days = (end - start).days + 1
is_full_year = days >= 365
incn_prev = INCN of the previous period (0/None if there was no period)
```

### 2. Choosing the rate

```
def rate_schedule(year, incn_prev, enc_window, startup_certified, patrimonial, days):
    if startup_certified:           return [(INF, 0.15)]
    if enc_window and not patrimonial: return [(INF, 0.15)]
    if patrimonial:                 return [(INF, 0.25)]
    if incn_prev is None or incn_prev < 1_000_000:
        t1, t2 = {2025:(0.21,0.22), 2026:(0.19,0.21)}.get(year, (0.17,0.20))
        tramo = 50_000 * min(days,365)/365          # prorrateo if the period is shorter than a year
        return [(tramo, t1), (INF, t2)]
    if incn_prev < 10_000_000:
        return [(INF, {2025:0.24, 2026:0.23, 2027:0.22, 2028:0.21}.get(year, 0.20))]
    return [(INF, 0.25)]

# enc_window: True in the first period with BI>0 and in the period right after it,
# if entity.is_new_creation and not activity_previously_by_owner_gt50 and not grupo42.
# State: enc_periods_used ∈ {0,1,2}; increment at the end of the period if 15 % was applied.
```

For "the first period with BI > 0", the decision is made after the BI (01330) is calculated. If BI ≤ 0, the ENC window does not start.

### 3. Modelo 200: the chain of casillas

```
C00500 = resultado P&G (after IS)                       # from the books: saldo 129 after 630
C00501 = resultado antes de IS                          # C00500 + gasto IS (630) 
ajustes_pos = Σ non-deductible items (art.15): sanciones, liberalidades, gasto IS(630 → C00301/00302 in 200),
              exceso amortización contable over fiscal, provisiones no deducibles...
ajustes_neg = Σ amortización fiscal acelerada above contable (ERD ×2, escaso valor, libertad)...
C00550 = C00500 + ajustes_pos − ajustes_neg             # BI antes RC y BIN

# Reserva de capitalización (only if C00550 > 0)
dFP = FP_fin − FP_inicio, where FP = capital + prima + reservas voluntarias (+ RC of past years),
      excluding resultado ejercicio, aportaciones socios, reserva legal/estatutaria, reserva nivelación
pct_rc = 0.20 (0.23/0.265/0.30 by Δ plantilla)
rc_derecho = max(0, dFP) * pct_rc + rc_remanente_anterior(≤2 years)
lim_rc = C00550 * (0.25 if incn_prev < 1_000_000 else 0.20)
C01032 = min(rc_derecho, lim_rc, C00550)
rc_remanente_nuevo = rc_derecho − C01032

# BIN
lim_bin = max(0.70 * (C00550 − C01032), 1_000_000)
C00547 = min(BIN_pendientes, lim_bin, C00550 − C01032) if C00550 − C01032 > 0 else 0
C00552 = C00550 − C01032 − C00547                        # Base imponible

# Reserva de nivelación (ERD, not ENC-15 %)
if C00552 > 0 and erd and not enc_window:
    C01034 = min(0.10 * C00552, 1_000_000 * min(days,365)/365)   # disminución (optional, by decision)
    C01033 = 0
elif C00552 < 0:
    C01033 = min(−C00552, niv_pendiente)                  # aumento: reversal of past reductions
    C01034 = 0
# + forced reversal of the remainder that has reached 5 years → C01033
C01330 = C00552 + C01033 − C01034                         # BI después de reserva de nivelación

# Cuota
C00558 = tipo(s)                                          # for micro: both tramos
C00562 = Σ_tramos max(0, min(C01330, lim) − prev) * t     # cuota íntegra (0 if C01330 ≤ 0)
C00582 = C00562 − bonificaciones − DDI (usually 0)
C00592 = C00582 − otras deducciones (usually 0)            # cuota líquida
C01785 = retenciones capital mobiliario (bank interest)
C01787 = retenciones arrendamientos (if the SL rents out property)
C01766 = Σ retenciones
C00599 = C00592 − C01766
C00601, C00603, C00605 = pagos fraccionados 1/P, 2/P, 3/P
C00611 = C00599 − C00601 − C00603 − C00605                # cuota diferencial
C00621 = C00611 (+/− rectificativa)                       # result: >0 ingresar, <0 devolver
```

Entries/reserves that the engine must propose after the result is approved: dotación reserva de capitalización = C01032; dotación reserva de nivelación = C01034 (both indisponibles); page 20 bis: RC/RN tables by year of generation.

### 4. Modelo 202: modalidad 40.2 (default)

```
def pago_202_402(mes, year):
    # the last 200 whose deadline expired by the 1st day of mes:
    ref_year = year − 2 if mes == 4 else year − 1
    m200 = filed_200[ref_year]           # None → base = 0 → do not file
```

### 5. Modelo 202: modalidad 40.3 (option; useful if the current year is worse than the last one)

### 9. Comparison of autónomo vs SL (2026, approximate)

Assumptions: expenses 6 000 €; RETA autónomo on the min. base of its tramo (≈ 1 274,51 €/month at 60 k, ≈ 1 732,03 at 120 k: 2025 bases, rate 31,5 %); RETA societario 1 424,40 € × 31,5 % paid by the SL; IRPF general scale ≈ estatal × 2 (19/24/30/37/45/47), mínimo personal 5 550; gastos difícil justificación 5 % ≤ 2 000; employee: −2 000 € + reducción; IS micro 2026 (19/21) or ENC 15 %; all profit after IS is distributed as dividends; fixed SL costs (RM, libros, depósito) 600 €/year.

| Scenario | Autónomo IRPF, net | SL micro, net (salary 20k / 35k / 50k) | SL ENC 15 % |
|---|---|---|---|
| 60 000 € | 37 078 | 35 666 / 36 222 / 35 609 | 36 571 / 36 652 / 35 609 |
| 120 000 € | 70 152 | 73 030 / 74 059 / 74 485 | 76 354 / 76 691 / 76 473 |

| Scenario (Beckham) | Autónomo Beckham 24 %, net | SL + Beckham (salary 20k / 50k), micro | ENC |
|---|---|---|---|
| 60 000 € | 37 379 | 31 739 / 34 724 | 32 643 / 34 724 |
| 120 000 € | 81 664 | 69 103 / 73 600 | 72 427 / 75 588 |

Rule of thumb: with ordinary IRPF, the SL is ≈ at parity at 60 k€ and gives +4–6 k€/year at 120 k€ with full payout. With reinvestment (profit stays in the SL), the gain grows by ≈ (ahorro rate × undistributed profit). Under Beckham the SL loses 3–12 k€/year if profit is paid out. It makes sense only as a "piggy bank" (deferred taxation) or for limited liability or client contract requirements.

## Edge cases

1. **Switching from autónomo → SL with the same activity**: 15 % ENC is not available (29.1 párr. 2: the activity was carried out by an individual who owns > 50 %). Engine: flag `activity_previously_by_owner_gt50 = true` → micro scale only.
2. **Socio-programmer and art. 27.1**: if the SL lives off his code and he is in RETA, his pay = a professional invoice (15 %/7 %, IVA), not a nómina. The position of administrador gets separate pay (it can be symbolic) with 35 %/19 %. Mixing both into one nómina is a classic mistake found in audits.
3. **Beckham holder and his own SL**: income as a professional through the SL = actividad económica → allowed only on the 93.1.b.3º ground (emprendedor; the Ley 14/2013 visa covers the ENISA requirement). On entry under 93.1.b.1º/2º (employment/administrador), such income is incompatible with the regime → take only the administrador salary (24 %) + dividends (ahorro). Details: the Beckham knowledge base page.
4. **Beckham and the region**: IS is a state tax. The ahorro scale is state-only. Beckham does not use the regional scale. Moving between comunidades autónomas affects only the administrador salary under ordinary IRPF (regional scale by residence on 31.12 / > 183 days).
5. **First period < 12 months**: the 50 000 € threshold of the micro scale and the 1 M€ limit of the reserva de nivelación are prorated by days. The 202 base for the next year is built by adding up periods up to 365 days.
6. **INCN = 0 in the first year** (no sales): for the rate of the next year INCN_prev = 0 < 1 M€ → micro scale. Retención of the administrador: 19 % if the period is closed and INCN < 100 k.
7. **Foreign clients and retención**: neither foreign nor Spanish clients withhold IRPF from an SL (unlike an autónomo-professional) → in modelo 200, retenciones = bank interest only. All IS is paid through 202/200.
8. **Loss in year N, profit in N+1**: no 202 is filed in N+1 (cuota N = 0). BIN is offset in the 200 for N+1 with no practical limit (< 1 M€). If the reserva de nivelación was applied in N−1, its remainder goes to C01033 in N.
9. **Undistributed profit**: it gives ΔFP → reserva de capitalización in the next year (20 % of the increase, limit 25 % of BI with INCN < 1 M€). It requires an indisponible reserve for 3 years. The engine must block its distribution until the term ends.
10. **Paying RETA from the SL bank account** without recording it as retribución → risk of requalification as a liberalidad (not deductible) and unreported income of the socio. The correct way: en especie through 640/4751 and in 190.
11. **Home office of the socio rented by the SL**: a vinculada operation at market value, retención 19 % (115/180), for the socio: rendimiento de capital inmobiliario. IVA: rental of housing by an individual is exempt, but premises used as an office are a taxable operation (risky; see the IVA knowledge base page).
12. **Foreign bank (Wise/Revolut) for the SL**: 720 is not needed if the account is recorded in the books with identification. It does not need to be stated in 036. Exchange differences: 768/668, subject to IS.
13. **Capital 1 €**: 20 % of profit goes to the reserva legal until capital + RL ≥ 3 000 €. On liquidation with debts, the socio is liable up to 3 000 €. With capital of 3 000 €: the usual 10 % up to 20 %.
14. **Modelo 232**: with payments to the socio-profesional > 50 % of INCN (operations of one type and method), it is mandatory in November even for small amounts. Penalty for not filing: informativa (min 300 €, 20 €/dato).
15. **Fixed 35 % in the first year**: the INCN of "the last closed period" does not exist → the < 100 k€ condition is formally not met → cautious: apply 35 % until the first closed year (see Open questions).
16. **Empresa emergente**: without an ENISA certificate entered in the RM, no benefit of Ley 28/2022 applies. The engine must require the certificate date and the registration note.
17. **Leaving Beckham / end of 6 years**: dividends and salary from the SL move to ordinary IRPF (regional scale for the salary). Nothing changes for the SL except the retención rate on the salary (24 % → 35 %/19 % or the general algorithm).

## Open questions

1. **Retención of the administrador in the first year of the SL**: there is no closed period to check INCN < 100 k€. UNVERIFIED (no DGT ruling found for this case). Cautious: 35 % until the first closed year. At year end it can be corrected only in the IRPF of the recipient (the overpayment will be refunded).
2. **IVA on the invoice of the socio-profesional to his own SL**: art. 27.1 decides only IRPF. The DGT makes IVA depend on "ordenación por cuenta propia" (risk, subordination). With 100 % control it is usually sujeto (21 %). For the SL it is deductible, so an error on the "with IVA" side is neutral. Cautious: invoice with IVA.
3. **Beckham recipient of salary/dividends: which retención models**: 111/190 and 123/193 (as for IRPF contribuyentes) versus 216/296 (IRNR). AEAT treats impatriados as IRPF contribuyentes with IRNR rules. Practice: 111/190 at a 24 % rate. UNVERIFIED for dividends; cautious: 123/193, 19 %.
4. **Prorrateo of the 50 000 € tramo in 202-40.3**: the instructions describe "tramos" by rate, but do not say whether the 50 k is prorated to 3/9/11 months. Cautious: apply the full tramo (as the AEAT form does with the entered rate) and recalculate in 200; UNVERIFIED.
5. **Start date of the first period** (escritura vs inscripción): DGT doctrine: IS from the date of the escritura for sociedades en formación that are then registered. Cautious: period.start = fecha escritura; check if there are more than 3 months between escritura and inscripción.
6. **Deductibility of administrador pay without a clause in the statutes**: the Supreme Court softened its position in 2023–2024, but AEAT still checks art. 217 LSC. Cautious: the engine requires the flag `estatutos_cargo_retribuido` + an acta of the socio único with the amount; otherwise a warning "risk of ajuste positivo".
7. **Rates for 2027–2029** are taken from the text of DT 44ª Ley 7/2024. AEAT so far publishes them only up to 2026. Future PGE/reforms may change them. Store them as a table by year.
8. **Verifactu**: legally 01.01.2027 for an SL. The announcement of 05.10.2026 about postponement to 10.2028 has no BOE publication. The Cortes were dissolved on 06.10.2026 (elections on 29.11.2026). Cautious: keep the Verifactu mode ready for 01.01.2027, with a switch-off flag for when the rule is published.
9. **RETA societario 2026**: 1 424,40 € is the final min. base after regularisation. During 2026 a provisional 1 000 € was allowed. The amount of the additional payment will come with the 2027 regularisation. Details: the RETA knowledge base page.
10. **IRPF scale in the autónomo vs SL comparison** is an approximation (estatal × 2). The real Comunidad Valenciana/Asturias scales differ by ±1–2 p.p. The 2026 RETA autónomo bases are taken from the 2025 table. The comparison figures are a guide, not a calculation for filing. Exact scales: the IRPF autónomo knowledge base page.
11. **Reserva de nivelación for micro on the two-step scale**: AEAT confirms that it applies (the scale is párr. 1 art. 29.1); for ENC at 15 % it does not. If the ENC window ends in the middle of the "life" of the reserve, the reversal continues under the normal rules. No guidance was found on this combination: UNVERIFIED.
12. **Hoja de titularidad real** is mandatory with the deposit under the models of Resolución DGSJFP 19.05.2026. A separate annual declaration to the RCTIR in January is **not** required for an SL (filing goes through the RM). If the RM rejects a deposit without the hoja → cierre registral.
